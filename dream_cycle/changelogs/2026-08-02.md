1. **Mask-first tradability design pattern** (arXiv:2507.07107) — appended a research-lead note to `wiki/trading/backtesting/lookahead-formal-verification.md`. Chinese A-share ML factor study documents a look-ahead bug class: non-tradable (price-limit-halted) closes leaking into rolling-window factor calculations before any tradability filter is applied. Their ablation: removing the mask cost -0.44 realized Sharpe and inflated in-sample IC by 18%. Flagged as a design-pattern audit lead for our own `run_hNNN.py` rolling-window signal calculations (12m momentum, IBS, drift gates), complementary to `survivorship-bias.md`'s universe-level delisting coverage — not a new backtest.
