## Applied (low risk)

1. **Grounded 8-K event taxonomy** (arXiv:2607.08346, Dolphin et al.) — appended a research-lead note to `wiki/trading/algorithms/pead.md`. Two-stage LLM pipeline tags 8-Ks into a 119-event, 3-tier taxonomy with verbatim-quote grounding; precision 12%→96% across quality-score buckets on 292,984 filings. Suggests H174's single FinBERT score>=0.18 filter may be blending economically distinct event types — flagged as a design lead for an event-type-conditioned filter, not a confirmed improvement (no ready-to-use package exists).
2. **Structured extraction beyond sentiment** (arXiv:2607.28496, Zhu et al.) — appended to `wiki/trading/algorithms/pead.md`. Verified numbers (financial news, not 8-Ks): FinBERT-alone F1=0.576, FinBERT+structured features F1=0.600 (p<0.0001), 53.5% disagreement rate between sentiment and structured signals — concrete evidence sentiment alone leaves signal on the table. Orthogonal design lead to proposal #1 (this adds dimensions on top of any event type, rather than reclassifying event type itself).
3. **Profit Mirage — information leakage audit** (arXiv:2510.07920, Li et al.) — appended to `wiki/trading/algorithms/llm-alpha-validation.md`. Methodological caveat: LLM-scored backtests can reflect pretraining memorization of historical price outcomes rather than causal signal. No hard FactFin performance numbers extractable from the abstract (not fabricated). Recommends a future audit of whether ProsusAI/finbert's training cutoff overlaps our H174 OOS window — audit lead, not a code change.
4. **Retail investor horizon PEAD overlay** (arXiv:2512.00280, Vamossy) — appended to `wiki/trading/algorithms/pead.md`. StockTwits holding-period cohort split: long-horizon investors underreact (PEAD), short-horizon investors overreact-then-reverse. Verified long-short alpha 0.43%/month (~5.16% annualized). Structurally orthogonal to H174's text/surprise gate — candidate third filter dimension, blocked on StockTwits data access.

All four proposals were low risk, append-only wiki research notes with no target-file backup required.
