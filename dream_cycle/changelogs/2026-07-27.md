## Key Insight This Cycle

Factor momentum IS the ML alpha signal on US equities. JPM April 2025 "Picking Winners in Factorland" finds that once factor momentum is controlled for, cross-sectional ML portfolios generate no significant alpha. This validates the H376/H395/IMOM family as the correct foundation — H456/H457 should be evaluated as enhancements to that base, not replacements.
