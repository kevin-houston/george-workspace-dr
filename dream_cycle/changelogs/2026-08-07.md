# Dream Cycle Changelog — 2026-08-07

**Applied:** 3 | **Skipped:** 0 | **Flagged:** 0

## Applied

1. **FinCAD** (arXiv:2605.24564, Li/Wang/Ma, University of Edinburgh) — inference-time Context-Aware Decoding fix for parametric look-ahead bias (LLM memorization of historical outcomes it's asked to forecast); cuts in-sample returns on memorized dates by up to -67.1% while leaving OOS Sharpe within ~0.10 of baseline. Caveat: needs decoding-time logit access, not available via George's frontier-model chat-completion APIs. Appended to `wiki/trading/backtesting/lookahead-formal-verification.md`. Low risk, wiki update.
2. **HindsightBench** (arXiv:2607.18867, Jia, University College Dublin) — black-box behavioral audit protocol for detecting parametric hindsight, no logit access needed (matches George's actual API access level); ~$19-30/audit row. Detection-side companion to FinCAD. Appended to `wiki/trading/algorithms/llm-alpha-validation.md`. Low risk, wiki update.
3. **Hyperbolic alpha decay from factor crowding** (arXiv:2512.11913) — candidate mechanism (game-theoretic crowding equilibrium, alpha(t)=K/(1+λt)) for the momentum degradation already flagged in H198/H435-H437. **Paper is withdrawn on arXiv as of 2025-12-27, "pending major revisions"** — flagged by dreamcycle-2026-05-29 peer review the same night before this build phase ran. The staged proposal and wiki content were both revised prior to apply (⚠️ withdrawn banner, hedged claims, risk bumped low→medium) rather than skipped outright, since the theoretical framing is still a useful hypothesis-generating lead per the reviewer's note — just not citable as a validated empirical result. Appended to `wiki/trading/backtesting/signal-halflife.md`. Medium risk, wiki update.
