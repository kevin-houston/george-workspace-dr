## Hypotheses Run

### H291 — 52-Week High Proximity Momentum (NOT CONFIRMED)

**Source:** George & Hwang (2004), *Journal of Finance*

**Signal:** R52 = P_t / max(P over prior 252 trading days); long top-10, monthly rebalance

**Results:**
- IS (2008–2017): Sharpe=1.031
- OOS (2018–2025): Sharpe=0.764, CAGR=11.6%, MaxDD=-14.4%
- WF=0.741, Corr(SPY)=0.731

**Verdict:** NOT CONFIRMED — OOS Sharpe 0.764 < gate 0.9

**Root cause:** In a 50-stock large-cap bull market (2018–2025), most stocks are near their 52-week highs simultaneously, collapsing cross-sectional R52 dispersion. Signal requires volatile/bear regimes or small/mid-cap universes.

**Script:** `backtesting/daily/run_h291.py`

---

### H292 — Return Seasonality (Same Calendar Month, 1-Year Lag) (CONFIRMED)

**Source:** Heston & Sadka (2008), *Journal of Financial Economics*

**Signal:** Return of stock in same calendar month of prior year → long top-10, monthly rebalance

**Results:**
- IS (2008–2017): Sharpe=0.688
- OOS (2018–2025): Sharpe=0.970, CAGR=18.2%, MaxDD=-19.1%
- WF=1.411 (OOS > IS — suspicious), Corr(SPY)=0.838

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED (both gates pass). ⚠️ survivorship bias caveat.

**Key finding — monthly breakdown:**
| Month | Mean Return | Win Rate |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Jul   | +5.10%      | 100%     |
| Nov   | +5.13%      | 88%      |
| Jan   | +2.69%      | 89%      |
| Sep   | -1.54%      | 50%      |
| Oct   | +0.08%      | 38%      |

July and November are the standout months — actionable as tactical overlays. Full monthly rotation not recommended due to weak months (Feb, Mar, Sep, Oct).

**Script:** `backtesting/daily/run_h292.py`

### H293 — Press Release Structure PEAD (Medium Risk) ✓ Applied

**Source:** arXiv:2509.24254, Wu et al., ACM ICAIF 2025

**What was applied:** New script `backtesting/daily/run_h293.py` (300+ lines).

**Key design:**
- Downloads EDGAR 8-K filings for 30 large-cap tickers (with disk cache)
- Splits press release text into 3 sections via regex: HIGHLIGHTS, RESULTS, GUIDANCE
- Scores each section with ProsusAI/finbert; fills missing sections with full-document score
- Composite: `0.3×s_highlights + 0.2×s_results + 0.5×s_guidance`
- Dual gate: composite ≥ 0.18 AND EPS surprise ≥ 0.02 (same as H174)
- IS: 2018–2022 | OOS: 2023–2025 | Gate: OOS WR > 81.8% (H174 baseline)

**Status:** Script ready; not yet run (EDGAR download ~30–60 min first run).

### H294 — Behavioral Multi-Factor MLP (Medium Risk) ✓ Applied

**Source:** arXiv:2508.14656, Yuqi Luan (2025)

**What was applied:** New script `backtesting/daily/run_h294.py` (290+ lines).

**Key design:**
- 27 OHLCV-derived behavioral factors across 5 groups: intraday structure (alpha101, IBS, range), volume-price behavioral (money flow, vol-price divergence, volume trend), momentum herding (rolling corr vol×return, multi-period returns), bottom-reversal (dist to 52w low/high, RSI, mean reversion), TA (MACD, Stochastic, ROC, vol ratio)
- Cross-sectional rank normalization per date (removes distributional drift)
- Dual-task MLP: input(27) → BN+ReLU+Dropout(64) → BN+ReLU+Dropout(32) → [return_head(1), direction_head(1)]
- Loss: 0.5×MSE(return) + 0.5×BCEWithLogits(direction)
- IS: 2013–2020 | OOS: 2021–2026 | Weekly rebalance (Friday → Monday)
- Gate: OOS Sharpe > 1.559 (H217 alpha101 baseline), long top-6

**Status:** Script ready; not yet run (needs OHLCV download + 80-epoch training).
