## 1. Earnings Footnote Complexity Signal
**Signal:** Ratio of footnote word count to total 8-K text length. Rising footnotes = management burying complexity. Inverse PEAD signal — above-average footnote ratio predicts post-earnings underperformance.
**Data:** EDGAR 8-K (already in pipeline). Parse using `sec-parser` or regex for Item 2.02 footnote sections.
**Mechanism:** Obfuscation theory (Li 2008, JF) — verbose/complex disclosures precede worse outcomes.

## 2. EDGAR Filing Timing Anomaly
**Signal:** 8-K filings submitted after 3 PM on Friday → statistically associated with negative news (weekend burial). Day-of-week + time-of-day of filing as a sentiment proxy.
**Data:** EDGAR ATOM feed timestamps (already used in PEAD intraday scanner).
**Mechanism:** Managers choose Friday afternoons for bad news disclosure (well-documented in empirical accounting literature).

## 3. Congressional Trading Mimicry
**Signal:** Replicate trades disclosed by US House members under STOCK Act with a 7-day lag. Members' net stock purchases (buys minus sells) by sector as a sector rotation signal.
**Data:** housestockwatcher.com API or quiverquant.com congressional trading data (free tier available).
**Mechanism:** Informed trading based on policy/regulatory access.

## 4. VIX Term Structure Slope Velocity
**Signal:** Rate of change of VIX9D/VIX ratio (9-day vs 30-day implied vol) over 5 trading days. Rapidly steepening contango (ratio falling) = near-term complacency → short-vol entry signal. Rapidly flattening (ratio rising) = fear spreading → exit short-vol.
**Data:** yfinance `^VIX9D` and `^VIX`. Both free.
**Mechanism:** More granular than static VIX level — captures the *direction* of fear, not just its magnitude. Complements H266 (VRP iron condor) with a dynamic entry gate.

---

## 5. Short Interest Velocity (Shorts Covering)
**Signal:** Week-over-week change in short interest as a % of float, not absolute short interest. Rapidly declining short interest (shorts covering) = contrarian momentum. Opposite: rising short interest velocity as a warning signal for longs.
**Data:** FINRA short interest data (published twice monthly, free) or Alpaca has some short interest data.
**Mechanism:** Short-covering creates forced buying pressure. Different from simple short interest ratio which many already trade.

## 6. Industry-Lead PEAD Propagation
**Signal:** When Company A in a 4-digit SIC code beats earnings by ≥10%, buy Company B in the same SIC code 2-3 weeks before B's earnings. Industry-wide demand/pricing tailwinds should propagate.
**Data:** EDGAR SIC codes (free) + yfinance earnings dates.
**Mechanism:** Same-industry earnings beats predict peer beats (well-documented in analyst research). Extension of H174 using inter-company information propagation.

## 7. FRED M2 Velocity → Value/Growth Rotation
**Signal:** When M2 velocity (nominal GDP / M2 money supply) is rising quarter-over-quarter, go long IVE (value ETF) vs. IVW (growth ETF). When velocity falling, reverse.
**Data:** FRED `M2V` (M2 velocity, quarterly) and `GDP` series. Both free via $FRED_API_KEY.
**Mechanism:** Rising money velocity = inflationary/growth environment where tangible assets outperform. Falling velocity = deflationary/QE environment where growth/duration outperforms.

## 8. Options Max Pain Pinning
**Signal:** In expiration week (Wednesday-Friday), stocks with unusually large open interest concentration at a single strike tend to pin near that strike (max pain). Fade moves away from max pain.
**Data:** Polygon options chain (free tier has some historical OI data, or Alpaca options).
**Mechanism:** Market maker delta hedging creates gravitational pull toward max pain strike near expiry. Well-documented academically but rarely backtested on a systematic per-stock basis.

## 9. Analyst Initiation Catalyst
**Signal:** New analyst INITIATIONS (not upgrades/downgrades — first-ever coverage of a stock) on mid/small-cap stocks. Initiations typically come with a price target and generate institutional attention. Buy at initiation, hold 30 days.
**Data:** FMP `/v4/price-target` endpoint includes analyst initiation data. $FMP_API_KEY available.
**Mechanism:** Coverage initiation increases institutional visibility → new buyers enter who were previously unaware of the stock.

## 10. CEO Option Cliff Vest Proximity
**Signal:** 4-6 months before a major CEO stock option cliff vest date, management has maximum incentive to boost stock price. Buy 5 months before cliff, sell at cliff.
**Data:** EDGAR DEF 14A (proxy statements) contain option grant/vest schedules. Free via EDGAR full-text search API.
**Mechanism:** Executive compensation incentives directly aligned with near-term stock performance before vest. Essentially front-running legal insider incentives.

## 11. Quarterly Earnings Revision Momentum
**Signal:** Stocks where analyst EPS estimates have been revised UP for 3 consecutive quarters outperform in the quarter following the third revision. Signal = streak of consecutive positive revisions ≥ 3.
**Data:** FMP `/v3/analyst-estimates/{symbol}` endpoint. $FMP_API_KEY available.
**Mechanism:** Systematic underestimation by analysts — once a company starts consistently beating, consensus estimates still lag reality, creating persistent upward revision pressure.

## 12. FRED Repo Stress → Size Rotation
**Signal:** When SOFR-Fed Funds spread widens (>20bps), small-caps (IWM) underperform large-caps (SPY). When spread narrows, small-caps outperform. Monthly rotation between IWM and SPY based on repo stress.
**Data:** FRED `SOFR` and `DFF` series. Both free.
**Mechanism:** Repo market stress indicates funding cost pressure on leveraged players (primarily small-cap oriented). Large-caps have direct Fed facilities access.

## 13. Cross-Asset Bullish Confirmation (Multi-Signal Gate)
**Signal:** Enter SPY momentum only when ≥3 of these 5 are simultaneously bullish: (1) TLT above 50-day MA, (2) GLD 3m return > 0, (3) EEM 3m return > 0, (4) DXY falling past month, (5) XLE above 50-day MA. When <3 confirm, hold cash.
**Data:** yfinance for all tickers. DXY via `UUP` ETF.
**Mechanism:** Rare simultaneous bullishness across diverse asset classes = genuine risk-on environment, not sector rotation. Reduces false momentum signals in choppy markets.

## 14. Patent Velocity / Revenue Signal
**Signal:** Companies filing a rapidly increasing number of USPTO patents per dollar of revenue → future stock outperformance (innovation leading indicator). Annual signal, rebalance yearly.
**Data:** USPTO PatentsView API (free, bulk downloads available). Match to company via CIK/EDGAR data.
**Mechanism:** Patent filings lead revenue by 3-5 years in hardware/biotech. Captures R&D quality without needing R&D spend data (which can be gamed).

## 15. Earnings Call Analyst Sentiment Shift
**Signal:** Use GPT-4o-mini to score analyst *questions* separately from management *answers* in earnings call transcripts. When analysts ask increasingly skeptical/negative questions but management gives positive answers → divergence signal predicts underperformance. When analysts sound impressed → outperformance.
**Data:** SeekingAlpha earnings transcripts (scraped) or EDGAR 8-K/10-Q. Build on H174's pipeline.
**Mechanism:** Analysts often signal their views through question framing even when not formally downgrading. This captures the unspoken analyst sentiment before it becomes a formal rating change.

## 16. Intraday Opening Range Expansion at Earnings
**Signal:** For stocks gapping up ≥3% at earnings open, measure how much the stock EXTENDS ITS OPENING RANGE in the first 30 minutes. Stocks that continue expanding (high > gap open) vs. fade (retrace to gap close). Extension = stronger 20-day PEAD signal.
**Data:** Alpaca 1-minute bars (paper account has access) or Polygon intraday data.
**Mechanism:** First 30-minute price action after an earnings gap reveals institutional conviction. Gap-and-go vs. gap-and-fade separates high-quality from low-quality catalysts. Upgrade path for H174 intraday entry.

## 17. CBOE SKEW Index Mean Reversion
**Signal:** When CBOE SKEW index (measures tail risk perception) rises above 145 and then reverses below 140, it signals that tail risk hedgers are unwinding → near-term equity outperformance. Mean reversion in SKEW → long SPY.
**Data:** CBOE SKEW CSV download (free, mentioned in VRP wiki page). Or yfinance `^SKEW`.
**Mechanism:** SKEW spikes when institutional players load up on OTM puts. Reversal = those hedges expire worthless or are sold, releasing capital back to equities.

## 18. Insider Net Transaction Score (Rolling 90-Day)
**Signal:** Rolling 90-day net insider transaction score per sector: sum (insider buy $ - insider sell $) / market cap, cross-sectionally ranked across S&P 500 sectors. Long top-2 sectors, short bottom-2.
**Data:** SEC EDGAR Form 4 ATOM feed (free). Already used conceptually in H186 (UOA).
**Mechanism:** Cluster of insider buying across an entire sector = sector-wide fundamental improvement. More signal than individual insider trades (less noise from personal liquidity needs).

## 19. Academic Citation Count as Stock Signal
**Signal:** Number of academic papers citing a company's patents (forward citations on Google Patents) → quality of IP signal. High forward citation velocity → future valuation re-rating.
**Data:** Google Patents API / Semantic Scholar API (both free). Annual signal.
**Mechanism:** Forward citations measure whether other companies are building on a firm's IP. High-citation IP = defensible moat. This is how VC firms informally assess tech companies.

## 20. Post-Split Price Momentum
**Signal:** Stocks that undergo a forward stock split (≥2:1) outperform in the 12 months following the split announcement (not execution). Signal: buy on split announcement date, hold 12 months.
**Data:** yfinance split history (free). EDGAR 8-K Item 5.02 for split announcements.
**Mechanism:** Companies only split when management is highly confident in sustained price appreciation. Split announcement = management's forward guidance signal. Well-documented but rarely systematized.
