# OpenKnowledge AI

AI agent-first notes app described as a productized version of Andrej Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" knowledge base concept.

- **Twitter/X**: @OpenKnowledgeAI
- **Source tweet**: https://x.com/daniel_mac8/status/2070827070117056883 (Dan McAteer, Jun 27 2026)
- **GitHub**: not yet located — check openknowledge.ai or @OpenKnowledgeAI on X

## What it does (from tweet)

- Import research papers, documents, and notes
- AI agent actively queries and synthesizes knowledge across the corpus
- Positioned as the "productized" version of the LLM-as-personal-wiki concept Karpathy described
- Demo: imported "Learning, Fast and Slow" (continual learning paper) and asked questions against it

## Relevance to Kevin

High relevance — this is essentially what George's wiki system does manually (ingest sources → build knowledge pages → query against them). OpenKnowledge AI is a product UI layer over the same concept.
