# J.P. Morgan Social Finance

A business unit within J.P. Morgan Securities (J.P. Morgan Chase & Co.) established in 2007 to:
- Invest proprietary capital in impact investments
- Provide client advisory services
- Produce analytical market research on the impact investing space

Organizational structure: separate team model (dedicated impact team alongside traditional investment teams).
