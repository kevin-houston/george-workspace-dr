## Cross-asset robustness validation (prompted by Kevin)

Kevin shared a key principle: a real strategy should work across multiple correlated assets, not just the one it was developed on. We ran formal cross-asset validation on H016 and H006.

### H016 (Momentum+Carry) — 5/6 universes pass ✓

| Universe | CAGR | Sharpe |
|----------|------|--------|
| SPY/TLT/GLD (original) | 12.9% | 1.10 |
| SPY/QQQ/IWM (equity only) | 13.3% | 0.64 |
| SPY/EFA/EEM (global equity) | 7.4% | 0.36 |
| QQQ/TLT/GLD (macro alt) | 13.2% | 1.08 |
| IEF/TLT/GLD (bonds+gold) | 8.7% | 0.81 |
| 5-asset macro top-2 | **14.9%** | **1.23** |

Key finding: signal is strongest where assets have *different* risk drivers. Pure equity universes degrade (high correlation = less discrimination). The 5-asset universe was the best performer.

### H006 (Dual Momentum) — 3/4 pass, conditional

Breaks on international/EM equity (Sharpe 0.18). Works on US sectors and factor ETFs. Safe-haven choice (BIL vs IEF) makes no difference. H006 is US-equity-specific.

## H018, H019, H020 — evening batch

**CONFIRMED**: 50/50 blend Sharpe 1.255, MaxDD -18.4%, daily correlation 0.31.

The two strategies are structurally uncorrelated: monthly macro rotation vs. daily oversold bounces. The 0.31 correlation is low enough to provide real diversification benefit — blending cut drawdown and improved Sharpe above both standalone strategies.
