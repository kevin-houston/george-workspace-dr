### H320 — LightGBM Crash Filter on H198 (PARTIAL CONFIRMED)

Applied LightGBM binary classifier (crash = monthly return < −5%) with 6 features (SPY/MA200, VIX level, VIX 20d change, momentum vol, momentum 6m return, T10Y3M slope) on H198 6-1m momentum. Rolling 24-month training window.

**Key insight: VIX simple gate HURTS** (OOS Sharpe 1.163 vs 1.207 baseline; 2022 MaxDD -26.9% vs -22.7%). It trapped capital in cash during volatile recovery months. LightGBM is smarter: 2022 baseline −10.2% → LGBM +5.7%.

**Variants C/D pass gate:** OOS Sharpe 1.274/1.283 > 1.174 baseline; MaxDD −14.4%/−15.0% vs −22.7% (8.3/7.7pp improvement). Zero negative years. WF 0.707/0.676 below standard 1.75 threshold (custom gate passes). Corr(SPY)=0.724 too high for direct production blend.
