# Research Log 2026-07-06

## Phase 1 — arXiv Scan

**Papers identified** (2025-2026):

1. **EarningsInOne** (arXiv:2606.29734, 2026-06-29, Yu et al.): First corpus aligning earnings press releases, ECTs, and intraday prices across S&P 1500 (2022-2025). Key finding: clean speed separation — EPS quantitative surprise eliminated by next open; ECT qualitative sentiment peaks *next trading day* and is real/tradeable. Prior ECT research missed this by using MSE instead of directional accuracy. Direct H174 upgrade path: H376.

2. **KTD-Fin** (arXiv:2605.28359, 2026-05-27, Zhu et al.): Memory-controlled benchmark for LLM trading agents on CSI 300 (2024-2026). Data-side masking + Barra attribution reveals that LLM agent returns largely decompose into market beta + style, not stock-selection alpha. Reproducibility template released. Warning for H280/H318.

3. **Strat-LLM** (arXiv:2605.06024, 2026-05-07, Huang & Yu): Stratified strategy alignment for LLM trading. Live-forward 2025 evaluation on A-share + US equities. Key findings: model scale non-monotonic for strict alignment; high-win-rate trap in standard LLMs; regime-dependent mode alignment. Connects to H249 regime framework.

4. **Network Momentum** (arXiv:2501.07135, 2025-01-13, Li & Ferreira): Combining univariate trend with cross-sectional lead-lag network momentum on 64 commodity futures, Sharpe 1.74 over 20 years. ETF adaptation feasible via rolling cross-correlation. H377 proposal.
