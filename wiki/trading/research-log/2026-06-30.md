**Research done:** Three 2025 papers from the Benhamou/Ohana group on CTA replication:

### Key findings ingested

**arXiv:2507.15876 — H262 basis (Bayesian Short+Long CTA Decomposition, July 2025)**
- Dynamic Bayesian graphical decomposition of CTA returns into: short-term trend, long-term trend, market beta
- Single-horizon benchmarks: 60d Sharpe 0.31 / 125d Sharpe 0.33 / **500d Sharpe 0.47** (most efficient)
- 500-day emerges as optimal single horizon: CAGR 7.2%, MaxDD 14.5%, Return/MaxDD 0.49

**arXiv:2510.23150 — Barbell Structure (Diversification Hides Redundancy, Oct 2025)**
- Medium-term (125d) overlaps with both short and long → removing it IMPROVES Sharpe and drawdown
- Optimal: 60d + 500d barbell only (skip medium-term entirely)
- Challenges conventional "more horizons = more diversification" assumption

**arXiv:2504.10914 — Cherry-Picking Warning (Valeyre, April 2025, revised Dec 2025)**
- Single EMA theoretically optimal for trend following under one-time-scale mean-reversion
- Test: if two signals have cross-correlation > 0.85, second adds no value
- Complex multi-indicator baskets improve historical Sharpe via backtest overfitting only
