### New content added

**New page**: `sources/alphazerobeta-market-neutral-rl-2026.md` — **AlphaZeroBeta:
Deep Reinforcement Learning for Market-Neutral Portfolios** (Boris Belyakov, HSE
University, arXiv:2607.18001, submitted 20 Jul 2026, accepted *Financial Innovation*
Springer). Personally verified via direct WebFetch on the arXiv abstract page (not
just subagent claims) before committing to it as content.

Core mechanism: CNN-GRU encoder → Recurrent PPO policy → weights projected onto an
**ℓ1-ball** (hard structural constraint forcing long/short balance) → composite reward
`risk_adj_excess_return − 0.5·corr(port,benchmark) − 0.001·turnover`. Tested on 7
equity indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ-100, DJIA, FTSE 100, DAX, Hang Seng, SSE Composite),
2014-2024, walk-forward across 22 rolling folds × 9 seeds (198 runs — a materially
more rigorous OOS protocol than a single split). Result: Sharpe 1.25 avg (vs 0.70 best
baseline avg across Index B&H / Max-Sharpe / Min-Correlation), Corr(benchmark) within
±0.15 across all 7 markets. The paper's own ablation is the standout finding: zeroing
the correlation-penalty term drops Sharpe 30-45% and lets correlation drift to 0.4-0.6
— proving the reward shaping, not RL alone, produces the neutrality.

Cross-linked into `algorithms/deep-rl-trading.md`'s "Honest OOS benchmarks" list
(alongside DDPG-TiDE, PPO+A2C+DDPG ensemble, and the cherry-picked TD3 example — flagged
as the more credible entry given its multi-fold walk-forward protocol) and into its
Cross-References section, plus links out to `strategy-blending-correlation.md` (the
production blend's stated unmet need for genuinely SPY-uncorrelated alpha — H311/H261b
are the best non-RL entries so far, both Corr(SPY) 0.2-0.5) and `llm-alpha-validation.md`
(the paper's Fama-French+Carhart+QMJ factor attribution is a discipline this wiki
regularly flags as missing from weaker RL/LLM trading papers).

Flagged a proposed next hypothesis in the page itself (not staged as a full H-number
tonight): scope narrowly to the H198/H241 S&P 500 universe (not all 7 international
markets), matched IS/OOS split, explicit post-cost Sharpe at 5bp/10bp, hard gate
Corr(SPY) ≤0.20 AND OOS Sharpe >1.0 — differentiator to prove is neutrality, not raw
Sharpe (which H417/H492/H493 already beat handily at 3-5+).
