### HIGH-RELEVANCE FINDINGS

**arXiv:2510.26228 (Oct 2025) → H279**
"ChatGPT in Systematic Investing — Enhancing Risk-Adjusted Returns with LLMs"
Anic, Barbon, Seiz, Zarattini
- LLM (ChatGPT) informed about upcoming momentum entry; filters based on news confirmation
- S&P 500 constituents; truly OOS after model pre-training cutoff
- Higher Sharpe and Sortino both IS and OOS; robust to transaction costs, prompt design, constraints
- Strongest for concentrated high-conviction portfolios
- **Directly upgrades H228 (alpha101+reversal blend, OOS Sharpe 1.572)** — adding LLM news confirmation as optional filter
- Staged as H279

**arXiv:2604.17327 (Apr 2026) → H280**
"Signal or Noise in Multi-Agent LLM-based Stock Recommendations?"
Fatouros & Metaxas
- MarketSenseAI: 4 specialist agents (News, Fundamentals, Dynamics, Macro) → synthesis agent
- Strong-buy EW portfolio: +2.18%/month vs +1.15% benchmark (S&P 500, 19 months, p=0.003)
- Cross-sectional IC: ICIR=+0.489, p=0.024 — statistically significant
- Live signal generation — no look-ahead bias
- Key insight: agent contributions rotate with regime (Fundamentals leads on S&P 500 normally; Macro dominates regime changes)
- Staged as H280 — replication/adaptation of 4-agent architecture for our universe

**arXiv:2606.08283 (Jun 2026) → H281**
"Macro Economists in the Machine: Multi-Agent LLM Framework for Commodity ETF Portfolio Construction"
Wang, Dai, Ma
- Hawkish/Dovish/Debate/Rule agents all receive identical FRED macro z-scores
- All 3 LLM agents outperform Rule Agent in Sharpe; Hawkish+Debate ΔSharpe = +0.044/+0.040 (p<0.10)
- Cost robustness: net advantage preserved up to 30bps one-way costs
- Debate Agent ≠ better returns, but = bias correction (averages out miscalibrated priors)
- **Very directly relevant to H026 commodity ETF rotation** — applying LLM macro interpretation to our existing FRED-based regime layer
- Staged as H281

**arXiv:2512.11913 (Dec 2025) → Wiki update**
"Not All Factors Crowd Equally: Modeling, Measuring, and Trading on Alpha Decay"
Lee
- Derives hyperbolic decay K/(1+λt) from game-theoretic equilibrium model
- Momentum: R²=0.65 hyperbolic >> linear 0.51 / exponential 0.61
- Crowding accelerated post-2015: factor ETF growth ρ=-0.63 with excess alpha loss
- KEY FINDING: crowding predicts TAIL RISK, not mean return — crowded momentum 0.38× crash probability, crowded reversal 1.7–1.8× crash probability
- Updates wiki/trading/backtesting/signal-halflife.md (adds hyperbolic functional form and crash-risk finding)
- Low-risk wiki update staged
