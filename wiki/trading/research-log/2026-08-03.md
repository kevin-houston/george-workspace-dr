### New content added

**New page**: `sources/spatio-temporal-momentum-2023.md` — full ingest of Tan, Roberts &
Zohren, "Spatio-Temporal Momentum: Jointly Learning Time-Series and Cross-Sectional
Strategies" (arXiv:2302.10175, *Journal of Financial Data Science* 5(3):107-129, Summer
2023). Selected because it directly answers the "H180 spatio-temporal momentum NN" item
already queued as a next research direction in CLAUDE.local.md — this closes that gap with
an actual paper rather than leaving it as a category placeholder.

Downloaded the full PDF via curl (poppler-utils/pdftoppm is not installed in this
container, so the Read tool couldn't render it directly; used the already-installed
PyMuPDF (`fitz`) library in the venv to extract raw text page-by-page instead — no new
package installed, per the standing off-hours install-security rule). Extracted and
verified concrete numbers directly from the paper text:

- **Core idea**: unifies TSMOM and CSMOM into one neural net trained with a direct
  annualized-Sharpe-ratio loss (not classification/regression) across four architectures
  (SLP, MLP, LSTM, CNN), benchmarked against a reference Deep Momentum Network (DMN) and
  classical Long Only/TSMOM/MACD/CSMOM.
- **Headline finding — simplicity wins**: on US equities (46 Financials-sector stocks,
  CRSP, OOS 1995-2022), vol-scaled OOS Sharpe — STMOM-SLP 2.609 (single linear layer) beats
  MLP 1.040, LSTM 1.015, CNN 0.192, and is competitive with the much larger reference DMN
  (2.920). On equity index futures the SLP-vs-DMN gap widens to >6x. Raw CSMOM (decile
  long/short) is actually unprofitable in this dataset (Sharpe -0.655) — consistent with our
  own H243/H271 finding that naive cross-sectional long/short underperforms long-only
  momentum at our universe sizes.
- **Transaction cost sensitivity (Table 6)**: unregularized SLP collapses from Sharpe 2.609
  at 0bp to 0.762 at 10bp; a turnover-regularized variant holds up much better (2.672 at
  0bp). Turnover regularization is the load-bearing implementation detail, not architecture
  choice.
- **Caveat flagged prominently**: both datasets (CRSP equities, Pinnacle futures) are paid,
  institutional-only sources — not directly reproducible with our yfinance/Alpaca/Polygon
  stack. Page proposes a concrete non-literal build path instead: swap in our existing
  `run_h241.py` 200-stock loader, keep the SLP architecture + Sharpe loss + turnover penalty,
  same IS 2013-2020/OOS 2021-2026 split as the rest of the H-series.
