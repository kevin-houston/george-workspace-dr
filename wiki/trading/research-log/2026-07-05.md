**New content added**:

1. **ACM Computing Surveys RL Survey** (arXiv:2408.10932, accepted April 2025): 167 papers reviewed. Key takeaway: no single RL algorithm dominates; actor-critic (PPO/SAC) best for continuous portfolio weights; financial non-stationarity = #1 failure mode; most papers suffer from single-seed variance and cherry-picked test windows.

2. **FinRL-X production upgrade** (github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL-Trading, 2026): Weight-centric architecture replacing FinRL 2020's monolithic gym coupling. Adaptive Rotation strategy: Sharpe 1.10 backtest (2018–2025), Sharpe 1.96 in 6 months live paper trading. Direct Alpaca integration eliminates H204 env build-from-scratch overhead.

3. **HMM + RL Regime Portfolio** (arXiv:2605.27848, May 2026): 3-state HMM + PPO policy layer on SPY/TLT/GLD, daily 2004–2025, 30% OOS. Extends our confirmed H249 (+0.282 Sharpe regime overlay) and H251 (3-state HMM). RL policy layer outperforms HMM-only. H371 candidate: replicate with BIL as 4th asset, IS 2004–2017, OOS 2018–2026.

4. **LambdaRankIC** (arXiv:2605.00501, May 2026): Direct Rank IC optimization via custom XGBoost objective. 60-year US equity dataset (21,396 stocks, 94 characteristics). IC=0.1148 vs OLS 0.0418 (+175%), ICIR=1.03, Sharpe=0.923. H370 candidate: apply to H198 30-stock universe using confirmed 6-1m momentum + alpha101 features.

5. **Updated H204 upgrade path**: LambdaRankIC (H370) has higher confidence and lower implementation risk — run first. H204 (full RL) reframed as Phase 2 after H370 result. H371 (HMM+RL regime) is parallel track for SPY/TLT/GLD universe.
