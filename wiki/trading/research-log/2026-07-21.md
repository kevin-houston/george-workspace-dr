## Phase 3: Look-Ahead Bias Correction (H411–H418)

**Triggered by**: dreamcycle-2026-05-29 flagged the extraordinary H417 results (OOS 5.855, MaxDD -1.2%) as suspicious.

**Bug**: In `run_h416.py`, `run_h417.py`, `run_h418.py` — the `backtest()` function used `signal.loc[month_end_T]` (drift gate computed from the last 20 daily returns of month T) to earn `monthly_ret.iloc[T]` (return FOR month T). The 20-day drift window covers essentially all of month T, so stocks with >60% positive days in month T almost certainly had positive T returns — selection-on-the-outcome, not prediction.

**Fix**: `backtest(monthly_px, sig.shift(1), top_n=n)` — use last month's gate to predict this month.

**Corrected scripts**: `run_h416_corrected.py`, `run_h417_corrected.py`, `run_h418_corrected.py` (`.shift(1)` applied).
