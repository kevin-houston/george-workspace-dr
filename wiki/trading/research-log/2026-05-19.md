### H206 — Halloween Effect (Sell in May) on SPY: NOT CONFIRMED

**Run**: `backtesting/daily/run_h206.py`  
**Results**: `backtesting/results/h206_results.json`

| Variant | Description | OOS Sharpe | Gate | Confirmed? |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------|-----------|
| H206-A | Hold SPY Nov–Apr, BIL May–Oct (~49% invested) | 0.535 | >0.6 | No |
| H206-B | SPY only in TOM windows within Nov–Apr (~10% invested) | 0.435 | >0.8 | No |
| SPY B&H | Benchmark | 0.789 | — | — |
| H201 ref | TOM-only (all months, ~19% invested) | 0.481 | — | — |
| H206-C diag | Summer TOM only (May–Oct) | **0.699** | — | — |

**Root cause**: Halloween Effect has deteriorated substantially in the 2018–2026 OOS period:
1. **2020 COVID crash** — H206-A was fully invested in March–April 2020 (winter months), capturing the crash fully. SPY +18.3% for the year; H206-A only +4.4%.
2. **Summer rally misses** — 2019 (+31.2%), 2021 (+28.7%), 2024 (+24.9%): H206-A captured only 50% of calendar, missing strong summer months.
3. **SURPRISE**: H206-C summer TOM (Sharpe 0.699) > H206-A Halloween (Sharpe 0.535). The TOM effect is actually **stronger in summer** than winter in modern data. Halloween filter reduces TOM quality rather than enhancing it.
4. **Monthly decomp**: November is the strongest winter month (+0.22%/day); Feb–Apr have near-zero or negative contribution.

**Conclusion**: Halloween Effect is not a viable overlay for the 2018+ period. The TOM filter (H201) is the correct calendar approach; adding Halloween restriction hurts it. H201 correlation to H206-A is only 0.332 — they diverge mainly in COVID and summer rally periods.
