### FRI Decomposition (arXiv:2606.29591)
Portnaya (Jun 2026) — "The Bounce Has No Direction." Develops Fourier-Residue Identity (FRI) that separates return autocorrelation into sign (directional) and magnitude channels. Key finding: SPY lag-1 reversal (ρ̂=-0.081, z=-7.4) is **entirely a magnitude effect** (bid-ask bounce) with no directional component (sign p=0.11). A real directional reversal appears at lag-3 (p=0.02) — a separate partial-price-adjustment channel. Cross-asset: mean reversion confined to equities and sovereign bonds; credit ETFs, commodities, FX, crypto are random walks. Validates monthly H198 horizon and weekly H181 setup.

### OFI as Cross-Sectional Signal (arXiv:2607.01377)
Aldridge (Jul 2026) — Kyle's λ from daily CRSP equity data (2020–2025). Signed order flow strongly predicts 1-month-ahead returns in Fama-MacBeth regressions. Volume volatility predicts lower subsequent returns (widening λ degrades price discovery). **Key insight**: Amihud ILLIQ ratio (|R|/DolVol) is a free yfinance-compatible estimator of this effect. Proposed **H393**: add Amihud ILLIQ rank as composite tiebreaker on H386 (IMOM+MOM).

### Optimal OFI Normalization (arXiv:2512.18648)
Kang (Feb 2026) — matched filter principle: institutional flows should use market-cap normalization; VWAP/TWAP flows use trading-value normalization. 1.99× signal correlation improvement from correct matching. Validates Amihud ratio as acceptable first-pass for institutional-flow cross-section.

### Hypothesis-Driven Microstructure Validation Framework (arXiv:2512.12924)
Deep et al. (Dec 2025) — rigorous walk-forward framework for microstructure signals on 100 US equities 2015–2024. Daily OHLCV microstructure signals: Sharpe 0.33, statistically insignificant (p=0.34), but strong regime dependence: +0.60% quarterly in high-vol (2020–2024) vs -0.16% in stable (2015–2019). Confirms OB filter in H343/H346/H386 is implicitly selecting for high-information-arrival regimes.

### AI-Driven Alpha Decay (arXiv:2605.23905)
Meng & Chen (Mar 2026) — calibrated to SEC 13F filings 2013–2024. Signal half-lives: momentum 84→12 months, value 72→20 months. Institutional portfolio convergence +42% since 2013. At AI adoption φ≈0.7, equilibrium signal half-life ≈18 months. Four theoretical results including Red Queen impossibility. IMOM's relative obscurity in public literature provides some decay buffer.
