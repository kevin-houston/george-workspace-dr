## Wiki Expansion: Factor Momentum & Style Rotation

New page: `algorithms/factor-momentum-style-rotation.md`

Created after running H255 (Factor ETF Momentum) and H256 (Dual Momentum / Antonacci GEM) — both
NOT CONFIRMED. The page synthesizes:

- **Gupta & Kelly (2019)** academic evidence: IS Sharpe 0.84 across 65 long-short equity factors
  worldwide; factor momentum is real but requires short-side capture
- **H255 failure analysis**: All 12 ETFs are US large-cap equity (Corr=0.894 vs SPY); no defensive
  escape during 2022 rate shock; long-only implementation cannot extract the short-side premium

  B&H (1.015) OOS 2015-2025; root cause = 2022 joint bond+equity crash (TLT −26%, SPY −18%)
  violating the strategy's core "bonds = safe haven" assumption; additionally, 12-month lookback
  too slow for post-2020 V-shape recoveries
- **Critical look-ahead bias trap**: unlagged 12-month momentum signal inflated GEM+Sector OOS
  Sharpe from 0.646 → 1.956 (3× inflation); always `.shift(1)` before using as trade signal
- **Extension path**: multi-asset 4-module structure (equity + credit + real assets + international)
  to provide multiple escape routes; H257 proposed
