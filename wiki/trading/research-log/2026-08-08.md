### New content added

Three new source-summary pages, all independently verified as real via direct WebFetch
to their arXiv abstract pages before citing:

1. **[ChatGPT as a Time Capsule](../sources/chatgpt-time-capsule-price-discovery-2026.md)**
   — Lehner & Lopez-Lira, arXiv:2604.21433. Frozen ChatGPT snapshots 2021-2025 as a
   point-in-time-safe historical text-information proxy; LLM outlook score predicts 1m
   cross-sectional returns (t=6.02); mechanism argued to be aggregation cost, not investor
   inattention. Distinct from H174's single-document FinBERT scoring — proposes a
   multi-document synthesis score as a complementary, not competing, signal.
2. **[From Hypotheses to Factors](../sources/hypotheses-to-factors-llm-crypto-2026.md)**
   — Huang, Fan, Hu & Ye, arXiv:2604.26747. A governance-layer paper, not a new discovery
   algorithm: point-in-time factor DSL makes look-ahead bias structurally inexpressible,
   plus an append-only experiment log. Crypto ridge portfolio trained 2020-2022, OOS
   2024-2026 44.55% annualized, Sharpe 1.55 net 5bp. Concrete transferable idea flagged:
   a `PITSeries.shift(1)`-enforcing wrapper class that would have structurally caught the
   H256 unlagged-signal incident.
3. **[Tail Risk Management with Puts and Trend Following](../sources/tail-risk-puts-trend-following-cvar-2026.md)**
   — Noguer i Alonso & Al Fallouji, arXiv:2607.00883. HJB CVaR framework blending OTM
   puts (instant protection on a jump) with trend-following overlays (lags the first
   shock, compounds defensive through a persistent drawdown). Filed as a Phase-2 tail-hedge
   overlay design candidate distinct from H273 (vol-targeting) and H320 (crash filter).
