- **Illusion Momentum (H385)** — Iwanaga & Hirose, Pacific-Basin Finance Journal Vol 96, 2026 (DOI: 10.1016/j.pacfin.2026.103063). Novel cross-sectional signal: ILLUSION = Σ(r_t) − [Π(1+r_t) − 1] = arithmetic sum of monthly returns minus geometric compound return. ## Phase 3: Nightly Backtest — H385 + H386 (Illusion Momentum family)

**Objective**: Implement and run the H385 stub (Illusion Momentum, staged 2026-07-08) and follow-up H386.

### H385 — Illusion Momentum standalone (PARTIAL CONFIRMED)

**Signal**: IMOM = MOM − SUM = [Π(1+r_t) − 1] − Σ(r_t), 6-month no-skip window on H198 30-stock universe.
**Result**: 1/5 variants pass gate. Standalone IMOM is high-alpha but fails MaxDD gate.

| Var | OOS Sh | MaxDD | Desc |
|-----|--------|-------|------|
| A | 1.704 | -53.5% | IMOM top-1 — fails MaxDD |
| B | 2.166 | -40.9% | IMOM top-2 EW — fails MaxDD |
| C | 2.258 | -31.3% | IMOM top-3 EW — fails MaxDD |
| **D** | **3.055** | -16.8% | **IMOM+MOM composite top-1 — PASSES** |
| E | 2.073 | -46.9% | IMOM+SPY 200MA — fails MaxDD |

Key insight: IMOM selects "smooth compounders" but these tend to be high-momentum names (TSLA, NVDA) prone to large drawdowns when concentrated top-1. The composite (50/50 IMOM + MOM rank) acts as a quality filter on momentum picks, improving OOS over pure MOM (3.055 vs 2.395 for H377 Var A) while controlling concentration risk.

Signal correlation rank(IMOM) vs rank(MOM_6_0): **0.436** — moderate, not redundant.

### H386 — IMOM+MOM Composite Weighting & Window Exploration (CONFIRMED, all 6 variants)

**Objective**: Test composite diversification (top-2/3) and weighting variations. Result: new best result in the H198 momentum family.

| Var | OOS Sh | MaxDD | Desc |

| **A** | **3.273** | **-7.5%** | **0.5×IMOM6+0.5×MOM60 top-2 — BEST** |
| B | 3.037 | -7.4% | 0.5×IMOM6+0.5×MOM60 top-3 |

| F | 2.983 | -13.4% | 0.5×IMOM6+0.5×MOM30 top-1 |

**H386 Var A vs prior best (H377 Var C)**:
- OOS Sharpe: **3.273 vs 3.075** (+6.4%)
- OOS MaxDD: **-7.5% vs -13.0%** (42% improvement)
- Annual returns 2022: **+83.5% vs -15.0%** (H386 completely avoids the 2022 drawdown)
- 0 negative years in both IS and OOS

**Research interpretation**:
1. Diversifying to top-2 (Var A) solves the MaxDD problem from H385 Var D (top-1) while *improving* Sharpe (3.273 > 3.055). The two stocks selected by the composite rank are naturally complementary.
2. MOM-dominant weighting (Var C: 67% MOM) outperforms IMOM-dominant (Var D: 67% IMOM) for Sharpe. IMOM is best as a tiebreaker quality filter, not the primary rank.
3. 12-month IMOM (Var E) is materially weaker (1.919 vs 3.273). 6-month is the natural window for both momentum and compounding-vs-arithmetic divergence.
4. IMOM signal adds genuine information beyond pure momentum (0.436 rank correlation) — it's capturing stock path quality/smoothness that predicts outperformance.
