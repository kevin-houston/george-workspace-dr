## New content: FCF yield ranking + value/momentum intersection magnitude

Added a new "§10. 2026 Research Update" section to `value-factors.md`:

- **Alpha Architect 40-year study (1971-2010)**: ranks Free Cash Flow Yield
  2nd-best of all single valuation ratios (~16.6%/yr top-decile return),
  behind only EBITDA/EV and ahead of B/M, E/P, and dividend yield — direct
  supporting evidence for the page's existing "FCF Yield — Dominant Modern
  Signal" framing (§3).
- **Value + momentum intersection, quantified**: a conjunctive AND filter
  (top-quintile FCF yield AND top-quintile 12-month momentum, not a rank-blend)
  produced **+506.3% higher cumulative return** vs. the FCF-yield-decile-alone
  baseline. This directly answers the page's own pre-existing "Open question
  #4" ("Value + momentum combo... intersection filter, reduces universe to
  ~5-8 stocks but higher precision") which had sat unresolved since the page
  was created.
