## New page: data-sources/databento.md

Databento was previously only a one-line mention in `tools/nautilus-trader.md`'s
supported-adapters table ("Data providers: Databento, Tardis, Blockchain") —
no standalone evaluation existed. Researched via `databento.com/pricing`,
`databento.com/equities`, and the vendor's own Python API demo blog post.

Key findings written up:
- **Coverage**: 50+ US equity venues (Nasdaq/NYSE/Cboe/MEMX/MIAX/IEX/FINRA TRFs),
  20,000+ symbols since 2018, plus CME/CBOT/NYMEX/COMEX futures+options with
  16+ years of history. Notably includes odd-lot trades (~50% of US equity
  volume) which consolidated SIP feeds — and by extension Polygon, Alpaca,
  yfinance — drop or aggregate away.
- **Pricing**: usage-based from $0.40/GB with no subscription required, or
  flat tiers at $199/mo (Standard), $1,750/mo (Plus, annual contract),
  $4,500/mo (Unlimited, annual contract). $125 free credit on signup,
  expires 6 months, one set per team.
- **Python client**: `pip install -U databento`, `Historical` client,
  `timeseries.get_range()` with dataset/symbols/schema/start/end params,
  `.to_df()` for pandas. Working example captured verbatim from the vendor
  blog.
- **Comparison table** against Polygon/Massive (`data-sources/polygon.md`)
  and Alpaca (`data-sources/alpaca.md`) on data type, entry cost, odd-lot
  coverage, options coverage, and best fit.
- **Verdict**: overkill for the current daily/monthly-rebalance H-series
  strategies — Polygon/Alpaca free tiers already cover the daily-bar use
  case at zero cost. Two concrete relevance points flagged instead: (1)
  H276 crypto POC / any future NautilusTrader live-execution work, where
  Databento is a first-class adapter; (2) if a future market-microstructure
  hypothesis (extending `algorithms/market-microstructure.md`'s OFI/Amihud
  work) needs genuine order-book depth instead of OHLCV proxies, the $125
  credit is enough for a `mbp-10` pilot pull before committing to a paid
  tier.
