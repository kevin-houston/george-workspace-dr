## New content: sources/finsaber-llm-investing-benchmark-2026.md

New source page on **FINSABER** (Li, Kim, Cucuringu & Ma, KDD 2026 Datasets &
Benchmarks Track, Oral; arXiv:2505.07078). The paper was already cited in passing
in `algorithms/llm-alpha-validation.md` (one sentence, no dedicated page) — this
gives it full treatment:

- **Methodology**: open-sourced backtest framework (`pip install finsaber`,
  Apache-2.0, github.com/waylonli/FINSABER, 143★, actively maintained — pushed
  2026-08-20) testing LLM timing strategies over 20 years / 100+ symbols, vs.
  the 1-2 year / <20-symbol scope typical of prior LLM-trading papers.
- **Finding**: previously-reported LLM advantages deteriorate significantly at
  that scale. Mechanism, not just an aggregate miss: LLM strategies are
  excessively conservative in bull markets (underperform passive buy-and-hold)
  and overly aggressive in bear markets (take losses a simple trend rule would
  avoid). Much of the literature's apparent edge traces to survivorship and
  data-snooping bias in small hand-picked universes.
- **Notable engineering detail**: the framework has a built-in rule — "date-only
  news or filing data should be treated as available no earlier than the next
  trading decision" — that is a structural guard against exactly the as-of-date
  look-ahead bug class George found by hand in the OB-filter family (H509-H514).
  Worth reusing as a design pattern if a from-scratch backtester is ever built
  for a text/filing-driven hypothesis (PEAD family, H316/H319 semantic pairs).

Cross-linked into `algorithms/llm-alpha-validation.md` (Test 3 citation now points
to the full writeup and states the bull/bear mechanism) and framed in the new page
as a 4th independent citation in the LLM-alpha-skepticism cluster, alongside Sheng
et al. 2605.16895 (Alpha Illusion), the price-vs-embedding EMH test (arXiv:2509.01590),
and George's own H520/H521 multi-agent degeneracy finding. Net effect: further lowers
the prior on H280 (MarketSenseAI) / H319 (LLM semantic network) being worth running
without a much narrower, mechanism-specific design — no new hypothesis proposed from
this alone, it's a benchmark/methodology paper not a strategy paper.
