## New content: MCPT Predictive Validity — 2026 Update

Added a new subsection to `backtesting/design-principles.md` right after the existing MCPT reference section, synthesizing three 2026 sources:

1. **Gatto (SSRN, March 2026)** — "Predictive Value of Within-Strategy Permutation Tests for Forward Selection." 437,911 strategy configs, 9 instruments across 3 asset classes, 160 walk-forward windows, 6+ billion permutations. Finding: within-strategy MCPT on path-dependent stats (MaxDD, Calmar, Ulcer) forward-predicts almost nothing beyond a simple IS-profitability gate. At the portfolio level, MCPT-smoothest in-sample equity curves *underperformed* OOS by −3.48pp — smoothness selects for overfitting, not robustness.
2. **Cha, Lee, Schrab & Kim (arXiv:2605.03886)** — permutation test statistical power is non-monotonic in permutation count due to discrete critical-value jumps; more shuffles isn't strictly safer.
3. **arXiv:2512.12924** — rigorous walk-forward validation case study (34 folds, MCPT p=0.98, honestly reported as a negative result) used as a template for what a well-specified, non-p-hacked validation pipeline looks like.

This directly revises design-principles.md's prior "Queued action: apply MCPT to all confirmed strategies as a retrospective audit" — that queued action implicitly treated a drawdown-family MCPT pass as informative for strategy selection, which Gatto's evidence contradicts. IS-profitability MCPT (testing return/profit-factor vs. permuted data, not curve smoothness) is unaffected and remains a reasonable secondary validation.

Added a short cross-reference caveat to `backtesting/multiple-testing.md`'s pipeline-application table pointing back to the new section.
