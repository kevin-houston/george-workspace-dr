### New content added

**4 new papers synthesized:**

1. **arXiv:2603.21892** — Keller-Ressel & Nikulski (2026). "Discovering parametrizations of implied volatility with symbolic regression." Uses genetic-programming-style expression search to discover analytic IV formulas without the SVI functional form assumption. Finds compact formulas that match SVI on in-sample and generalize better at extreme strikes. Added as Paper 3.

2. **arXiv:2509.01743** — Wang, Liu & Vuik (2025). "Controllable Generation of Implied Volatility Surfaces with Variational Autoencoders." VAE with disentangled latent space (vol level, skew slope, curvature, term structure vs. residual). Explicit shape-feature control makes this the right choice for risk-scenario generation and surface completion. Added as Paper 4.

3. **SSRN:4531181** — Kelly, Kuznetsov, Malamud & Xu (2023, rev. 2025). "Deep Learning from Implied Volatility Surfaces." Full IV surface (all strikes × maturities) contains alpha for cross-sectional equity returns, incremental to standard factors. ATM IV, IV slope (skew), and term-structure slope are the three highest-information features. This is the fundamental academic grounding for H449.

4. **JFM 2026** — Duan et al. "Option Implied Volatility and Trading Strategies Based on Neural Network Correction." Two-stage hybrid: (1) fit SABR/SVI; (2) NN residual correction. Reduces IV RMSE ~15–20% vs pure SABR; most pronounced for short-dated OTM. Provides H448 Var B path (cheaper than full DDPM, better than plain EWMA).
