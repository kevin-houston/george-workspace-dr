## H155 — TLT/IEF Kalman Filter

**NOT CONFIRMED** — Worse than static OLS in all variants.

### What was tested

- Pair: TLT/IEF (same as H154)
- Kalman state-space model: β_t = β_{t-1} + η, y_t = β_t·x_t + ε
- 6 variants: Q = {1e-5, 1e-4, 5e-4, 1e-3} (adaptation speed), z_lb = {30d, 60d}, with/without drifting intercept α
- Benchmark: H154 Variant A (static OLS, z_lb=30d) OOS Sharpe=0.514

### Results

| Variant | Q | z_lb | OOS Sharpe | OOS Cumul | Spread HL (OOS) |
|---------|---|------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| A | 1e-4 | 30d | −0.051 | 0.985× | 1.1d |
| B | 1e-3 | 30d | −0.110 | 0.970× | 0.7d |
| C | 1e-5 | 30d | **0.118** | 1.042× | 2.1d |
| D | 1e-4 | 30d | −0.096 | 0.977× | 1.1d |
| E | 1e-4 | 60d | 0.013 | 1.004× | 1.1d |
| F | 5e-4 | 30d | 0.092 | 1.025× | 0.8d |

H154 static OLS benchmark: OOS Sharpe=0.514, cumul=1.226×.

**Root cause**: The Kalman filter is self-defeating. As Q increases, β adapts faster → spread half-life collapses → nothing to trade. Even the slowest variant (C, Q=1e-5) achieves only HL=2.1d (vs 30d needed for z-score signals to fire). The 91–94% flat time tells the story: the strategy barely enters any positions.

**Key insight**: TLT/IEF spread profitability in H154 comes from a *fixed* relationship with short-term deviations. Making β adaptive removes those deviations by construction. This is an important structural insight about pairs trading: the signal lives in the residual from a *static* model. If you let the model track the relationship in real time, the residual becomes noise.

**Conclusion**: ETF pairs trading family is fully exhausted. 4 tests, 0 confirmed:

| H# | Pair | Methodology | Result |
|----|------|-------------|--------|
| H152 | GDX/SIL | Rolling OLS | NOT CONFIRMED (OOS coint fails) |
| H153 | XLE/OIH | Rolling OLS | NOT CONFIRMED (β negative, pair broken) |
| H154 | TLT/IEF | Static OLS | NOT CONFIRMED (no formal coint, but Sharpe 0.514) |

## H156 — Cross-Sectional Stock Momentum (NASDAQ)

**PARTIAL** — Genuine momentum signal but doesn't beat QQQ.

### What was tested

- Universe: 54 large-cap NASDAQ stocks with pre-2008 history (survivorship-biased)
- Signal: 12-1 month momentum (Jegadeesh-Titman), skip 1 month to avoid reversal
- Top-N ranking, equal-weight, monthly rebalance
- 0.1% RT transaction cost
- Benchmark: QQQ (4.48× OOS), SPY (3.03× OOS)

### Performance

| n_hold | IS Sharpe | OOS Sharpe | OOS Cumul | OOS MaxDD | AltOOS Sharpe |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| 10 | 2.420 | 0.541 | 3.04× | −37.0% | 1.012 |
| 15 | 2.537 | 0.754 | 3.92× | −28.9% | 1.207 |
| **20** | **2.541** | **0.866** | **4.18×** | **−29.7%** | **1.295** |

QQQ OOS: 4.48× Sharpe=0.983. SPY OOS: 3.03× Sharpe=0.861.

Best variant n=20 beats SPY but not QQQ. AltOOS Sharpe of 1.295 is strong.

**OOS correlation with SPY**: 0.773 — high equity beta, not independent alpha.

### Caveats

1. **Survivorship bias**: Universe is current large-caps that survived to 2026. Companies that went bankrupt, got acquired, or dropped out of NASDAQ-100 are excluded. True unbiased returns would be lower — possibly by 2-4% annually based on literature.

2. **High MaxDD**: −29.7% on n=20 is far worse than H149's −9.6%. Stock momentum drawdowns are driven by momentum crashes (2020 COVID reversal, 2022 factor rotation).

3. **High correlation**: 0.773 with SPY means this adds mostly equity beta exposure, not independent alpha for the portfolio.

### What this tells us

The momentum signal is real — 12-1 month momentum in large-cap NASDAQ stocks produces Sharpe 0.866 OOS with survivorship bias (likely 0.5-0.7 after bias correction). But the execution challenges:
- Universe survivorship bias inflates returns
- High MaxDD requires careful sizing
- Low diversification value vs existing H026/H149 portfolio

### Next steps

**H157 options** (priority order):

1. **Sector-neutral momentum**: Rank stocks within their GICS sector, hold top-N per sector. Reduces market beta and momentum crash risk. Requires broader universe.

2. **Factor momentum (ETF)**: Instead of individual stocks, use 30+ factor/style ETFs (e.g., RPG/RPV for growth/value, IWF/IWD, etc.) with the same 12-1 signal. Bias-free, lower turnover, no survivorship issue.

3. **QQQ basket rotation**: Trade a fixed universe of QQQ-like stocks but ranked by a combined momentum+quality score. Quality filter reduces crash exposure.
