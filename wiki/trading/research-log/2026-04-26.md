## Key findings

### Strategy rankings (evidence quality)
1. **Iron condor** — strongest evidence; tastytrade 14-year backtest, 78-83% win rate at 16-delta 45-DTE; well-documented management rules (close at 50% profit, exit at 2× loss, roll at 21 DTE)
2. **Put spread selling** — 93% win rate in 2024 backtests (diagonal puts); tastytrade theta/vega ≥ 30% entry filter is the key edge metric
3. **Covered calls** — moderate evidence; CBOE BXM index underperforms buy-and-hold long-term due to capped upside; better for flat/bearish environments or tax-deferred accounts
4. **VRP harvesting** — highest potential (Sharpe 1.19 vs. 0.33 buy-and-hold per AQR) but extreme tail risk; requires VIX call hedges to be viable; not suitable as first strategy

### Wheel strategy finding (important)
spintwig backtest on SPY: 94-99% of wheel returns come from the underlying stock position, not options premium. Wheel frequently **underperforms buy-and-hold** on SPY. Works better on high-IV individual stocks where the premium is substantial enough to justify the complexity.

### Critical backtesting discipline for options
Options-specific pitfalls beyond standard equity backtesting:
- Bid-ask slippage: 75% of spread for 1-leg, 56% for 4-leg (understating this is the #1 error)
- Path dependency: model overlapping positions simultaneously (not one-at-a-time)
- Volatility regime testing mandatory: strategies that worked 1990-2007 blew up 2008
- IV entry filters matter more than strike/DTE choice
