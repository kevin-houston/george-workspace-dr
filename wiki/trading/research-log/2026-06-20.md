## Phase 1: Wiki Expansion

**New page:** [`algorithms/fixed-income-bond-rotation.md`](../algorithms/fixed-income-bond-rotation.md)

The bond/fixed-income section was the clearest gap — H045 is 21% of the production portfolio with OOS Sharpe 1.351 but had no dedicated wiki page documenting the theoretical framework, signal design, or failure analysis.

**Key findings documented:**
- **Signal design confirmed:** 3m+6m+12m rank ensemble + inverse vol tiebreaker. Monthly rebalance.
- **3-month TSMOM filter is the key protective mechanism** — exited TLT early in 2022 rate shock, limiting H045 MaxDD to −6.3% vs. AGG −13%.
- **Carry FAILS on ETF dividends:** H283 all variants Sharpe ≤ 0.743 (below gate). Root cause: yfinance dividends ≠ forward-looking bond carry (academic carry uses term premium, repo rates). Stick to momentum signals only.
- **SHY dominates OOS** (72% of months held) — 2017–2026 was a rising-rate regime where short duration systematically outperformed.
- **TLT essentially never held** (<1% of OOS months) — the strategy's avoidance of duration risk in rising-rate cycles is the primary OOS alpha source.
- **Bond-equity correlation flip:** Normal regime −0.2 to −0.4; rate shock (2022) +0.5 to +0.8. Bonds don't hedge equities when rates are the primary driver.
- **Queued hypotheses:** H314 (FRED term premium as duration-factor overlay), H315 (HY credit spread regime gate for universe filtering).
