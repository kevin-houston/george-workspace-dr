### New content added

**New page**: `prediction-markets/backtesting-frameworks.md` — before writing, read
the existing `other-platforms.md` in full to check for overlap. Its "Open Datasets &
Unified APIs" section already covers jon-becker's 36GB dataset and pmxt's unified SDK
thoroughly (added 2026-07-28), so those were **not** re-covered. The genuine gap
identified: no wiki coverage of open-source **backtest engines** or **agent trading
frameworks** purpose-built for prediction markets — a different layer from the raw
data/unified-API tooling already documented.

Covered 4 repos found via WebSearch + WebFetch on their READMEs:

- **Quentin-Piot/prediction-market-backtester** (MIT, 6★) — quant-style Polymarket+Kalshi
  backtester with an explicit ask-fills-buys/bid-fills-sells convention, configurable
  fee/slippage/latency model, and gross-cash-at-risk exposure limits. No order-book depth
  or partial-fill modeling yet. Flagged the fill convention as worth adopting for any
  future H185 PM backtest harness, matching our own `design-principles.md` transaction-cost
  discipline.
- **evan-kolberg/prediction-market-backtesting** (mixed MIT/LGPL-3.0, 1.1K★, 181 forks) — a
  NautilusTrader extension. Polymarket fully supported (live sandbox for BTC 5-min markets);
  Limitless/Opinion planned; **Kalshi blocked** — no path forward without L2 historical
  order-book data, which isn't available upstream. Noted this as a known dead end for
  order-book-level Kalshi backtesting generally, not a data problem specific to us —
  `PredictionMarketBench` (already in `ai-model-benchmarks.md`) remains the working Kalshi
  path via trade-tape replay.
- **cryptuon/polybot** (MIT, 8★) — agent-first trading framework: 25+ typed MCP tools,
  Claude Code Skill, paper-trading default with mandatory human approval before any live
  order, position/loss/exposure limits enforced platform-wide. Flagged as the closest prior
  art to "give George native Kalshi/Polymarket tools with paper-default + human-approval,"
  worth revisiting if H185 graduates from nowcasting research into live execution.
- **ImMike/polymarket-arbitrage** — watches 10,000+ markets for Polymarket↔Kalshi pricing
  gaps via text-similarity market-title matching rather than a hand-maintained ticker pair
  list, a meaningfully different scaling approach from what `other-platforms.md`'s existing
  cross-platform-arb section implies.

Updated `wiki/trading/index.md` frontmatter (`updated`/`prev`) and the Prediction Markets
section entry list.
