Covered 4 repos found via WebSearch + WebFetch on their READMEs:

- **Quentin-Piot/prediction-market-backtester** (MIT, 6★) — quant-style Polymarket+Kalshi
  backtester with an explicit ask-fills-buys/bid-fills-sells convention, configurable
  fee/slippage/latency model, and gross-cash-at-risk exposure limits. No order-book depth
  or partial-fill modeling yet. Flagged the fill convention as worth adopting for any
  future H185 PM backtest harness, matching our own `design-principles.md` transaction-cost
  discipline.
- **evan-kolberg/prediction-market-backtesting** (mixed MIT/LGPL-3.0, 1.1K★, 181 forks) — a
  NautilusTrader extension. Polymarket fully supported (live sandbox for BTC 5-min markets);
  Limitless/Opinion planned; **Kalshi blocked** — no path forward without L2 historical
  order-book data, which isn't available upstream. Noted this as a known dead end for
  order-book-level Kalshi backtesting generally, not a data problem specific to us —
  `PredictionMarketBench` (already in `ai-model-benchmarks.md`) remains the working Kalshi
  path via trade-tape replay.
- **cryptuon/polybot** (MIT, 8★) — agent-first trading framework: 25+ typed MCP tools,
  Claude Code Skill, paper-trading default with mandatory human approval before any live
  order, position/loss/exposure limits enforced platform-wide. Flagged as the closest prior
  art to "give George native Kalshi/Polymarket tools with paper-default + human-approval,"
  worth revisiting if H185 graduates from nowcasting research into live execution.
- **ImMike/polymarket-arbitrage** — watches 10,000+ markets for Polymarket↔Kalshi pricing
  gaps via text-similarity market-title matching rather than a hand-maintained ticker pair
  list, a meaningfully different scaling approach from what `other-platforms.md`'s existing
  cross-platform-arb section implies.

Updated `wiki/trading/index.md` frontmatter (`updated`/`prev`) and the Prediction Markets

**5 proposals staged** at `dream_cycle/staged/2026-08-05/` (files 4-8):

4. **FinSMART RL-trained sentiment scoring** (wiki_update, low risk) — arXiv:2607.28127,
   trains a sentiment scorer via RL directly against realized market returns instead of
   FinBERT's static human-labeled classification. +220% cumulative return over strongest
   baseline claimed (no Sharpe/WR disclosed). Parallel track to H481 (FinDPO) — targets
   replacing the H163/H174 scorer itself rather than adding filters on top, which is where
   every recent attempt (H175/H317/H469) has plateaued. Appended to `algorithms/pead.md`
   as a research lead, not yet a hypothesis.
5. **Koijen/Levy NBER agentic earnings-signal variance** (wiki_update, low risk) — NBER WP
   w35431, Chicago Booth. Agentic AI explains ~17-20% of same-day earnings-announcement
   price variance vs ~5-8% for EPS-surprise-only, on ~2,000 real 2025 earnings events.
   Corroborates the H163/H174 "text beats surprise-alone" thesis at a different task
   (same-day variance vs multi-week drift). Paywalled beyond abstract — flagged low-to-medium
   confidence. Appended to `algorithms/llm-alpha-validation.md`.
6. **Dikhit conditional skip-month momentum** (new_script, medium risk) — Zenodo preprint,
   Jan 2026. Finds skip-month isn't universally beneficial: when the prior month's return
   was above the stock's own trailing average, including it actually helps rather than
   hurts. Directly engages the H277 anomaly (skip-month hurts on NASDAQ tech momentum).
   Staged as an **H491 stub** at `backtesting/daily/run_h491_conditional_skip_momentum.py`
   (raises NotImplementedError — needs universe/gate decisions before it can run; flagged
   for manual build-out rather than 4am auto-apply, consistent with medium-risk policy).
7. **TradeLens agent cost-attribution diagnostic** (wiki_update, low risk) — arXiv:2607.10286.
   Not another architecture proposal — a diagnostic toolkit attributing P&L to specific
   agent decisions and auditing whether LLM inference cost is justified by incremental
   profit. Maps onto the existing Coordination Breakeven Spread (CBS) metric already in
   `multi-agent-llm-trading.md`; directly applicable once H274's 3-agent PEAD debate goes
   live. No Sharpe/return numbers disclosed — methodology-only.
8. **cvxportfolio cost-aware convex optimization** (wiki_update, low risk) —
   github.com/cvxgrp/cvxportfolio, Stanford Boyd group, 1,246★. Fills a real gap: existing
   portfolio-optimization coverage (PyPortfolioOpt/Riskfolio-Lib/skfolio) handles convex
   optimization only, none combine it with a cost-aware backtest loop (costs baked into the
   optimization objective per-rebalance rather than applied post-hoc). Testable against
   H026/H045/H041a's current top-N rotation logic. Appended to `tools/portfolio-optimization.md`.
