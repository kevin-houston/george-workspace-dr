# 2026-08-09 — Prediction Markets refresh (Kalshi + Polymarket)

## Why this section

Reviewed `wiki/trading/index.md` page counts to find the least-covered section. Prediction Markets (9 sub-pages) was the actionable choice over the raw-thinnest Sources section (6 sub-pages, but overlapping with tonight's Phase 2 arXiv scan angles). Both flagship pages, kalshi.md and polymarket.md, hadn't been updated since 2026-04-29 — over three months stale — and 2026 H2 brought major, concrete developments on both platforms.

## What changed

**kalshi.md**:
- Rewrote the volume/scale picture: June 2026 monthly volume hit **$31B**, up 70%+ from May's $17.9B, driven by the FIFA World Cup ($22.42B World-Cup-specific volume, ~3M new users, >$1B/day sustained). Industry-wide (all platforms) monthly volume crossed $50B in June 2026 — reportedly surpassing traditional sportsbook handle for the event. This dwarfs the "early 2026" $4.5B/month baseline previously on the page (kept for historical reference, not deleted).
- Added a new **Regulatory Risk** section: Minnesota's felony statute for operating a prediction market platform (effective Aug 2026), a federal judge ruling that Kalshi's *sports* contracts remain subject to Utah's anti-gambling law (a specific carve-out — economic/political contracts are on firmer ground), CFTC's April 2026 lawsuit against Arizona/Connecticut/Illinois seeking federal preemption, and market-implied ~64% odds of a SCOTUS case by year-end. Practical takeaway for this project: stick to economic/nowcasting contracts (CPI/Fed/jobs), which sit on firmer legal footing than the sports category currently under state-level pressure.

**polymarket.md**:
- Added the QCEX acquisition backstory: Polymarket bought the CFTC-licensed QCEX DCM + its clearing arm for $112M (July 2025), which is the regulatory vehicle behind the Nov 2025 CFTC Amended Order of Designation already on the page. Added the timeline: Dec 2025 invite-only relaunch → March 2026 self-certification of US market rules → May 2026 waitlist removal (iOS only; Android/web still pending as of this check).
- Updated the "V2 upgrade (announced)" line to confirmed-live as of April 28, 2026, 11:00 UTC — named the new settlement token **pUSD** (replaces USDC.e), noted the expanded 23 REST + 2 WebSocket API surface and Python/TypeScript SDKs.
- Updated the Liquidity vs. Kalshi table with Kalshi's new $31B figure, but flagged that no directly comparable Polymarket June 2026 monthly volume figure was found — logged as an open follow-up rather than guessed.
