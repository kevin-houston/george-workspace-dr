## New content: Options Flow, Dealer Positioning & Dark Pool Data

New page `data-sources/options-flow-positioning-data.md`. Real gap: `options-data.md` covers historical options *chain* data (ThetaData/ORATS/Polygon Greeks/IV) and `alternative-data.md` covers Quiver's congressional/social/short-volume feeds, but neither covers the derived-positioning-signal category — unusual options flow alerts, dealer gamma exposure (GEX/DEX/VEX), dark pool prints — despite `algorithms/options-income-strategies.md`'s existing 0DTE iron condor playbook explicitly gating entries on "FlashAlpha or equivalent" dealer GEX risk score.

Researched via WebSearch/WebFetch (unusualwhales.com/public-api, api.unusualwhales.com/docs, flashalpha.com articles):

1. **Unusual Whales** — 200+ REST endpoints + 15 WebSocket channels + hosted MCP server; flow, dark pool, GEX/Greeks, congress/insiders in one API. No permanent free tier (1-week trial only, 30k req/day). Paid entry $150/mo (40k req/day, 2yr lookback); full-market historical option trades priced separately at $250/mo.
2. **FlashAlpha** — pre-computed per-strike dealer gamma/delta/vanna, gamma flip, call/put walls, regime classification. Genuinely free tier: 5 req/day, no card required — confirmed viable for the existing options-income-strategies.md single-ticker daily GEX check. **Pricing correction**: `options-data.md` (last touched 2026-05-01) lists FlashAlpha as "Free / $79 / $299" — actual current tiers are $0 / $63 / $239 / $1,199/mo. Flagged in the new page for a future options-data.md reconciliation pass (not made tonight — out of scope for a single-page addition).
3. **SpotGamma** — strongest discretionary brand (HIRO indicator), but no public documented API. Logged as reference-only / not usable for this project's automated pipeline.
4. **Quiver Quantitative** — already covered in alternative-data.md; cross-referenced its `offexchange()` dark-pool-%-of-volume endpoint against Unusual Whales' finer-grained print-level dark pool endpoints.

Verdict written into the page: FlashAlpha free tier already covers the existing 0DTE playbook's need; Unusual Whales positioned as a discretionary confirmation layer, not a systematic backtest input, absent evidence the flow signal adds edge beyond the existing GEX regime classification.

Cross-linked into the new page from options-data.md's category, and referenced options-income-strategies.md, spx-dispersion-variance.md, and smart-money-concepts-ict.md as downstream consumers.
