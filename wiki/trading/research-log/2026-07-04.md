### Research Conducted

Searched for new papers in Bruce Dean's information geometry series. Found two important additions:

1. **SSRN 6637139** — "The Geometric Volatility Smile with Jumps: A Closed-Form Three-Term Decomposition" (April 23, 2026)
   - Extends SSRN 6630259 by separating κ_eff into three structural terms
   - κ_eff(T) = κ_stochvol + κ_leverage + κ_jumps(T)
   - Jump contribution: Gram-Charlier expansion of Merton compound-Poisson model
   - Addresses the "below ~15 days, jumps dominate" limitation of the prior paper
   - Connects to SABR (β=1 is a structural prediction from Čencov's theorem, not a free parameter)

2. **arXiv:2603.14438** — "Curved Greeks: A Geometric Layer for Option P&L Adjustments" (March 15, 2026)
   - Independent group (Pérez Velasco, Lu, Arrieta) working in the same geometric framework
   - Standard Gamma/Hessian depends on pricing coordinates → model arbitrage in risk books
   - Fix: replace Hessian with covariant Hessian (affine connection) → coordinate-invariant second-order P&L
   - Applied to FX barrier options (EURUSD, USDTRY); relevant to H309 dispersion and H266 iron condor

### Key Takeaways

The Dean series is maturing into a complete geometric theory of options pricing. The practical hierarchy is now clear:
- LEAPS (>1yr): use two-term formula with ρ/ν/σ₀ from time series → skew within 19%
- Monthly options (15d-1yr): add jump correction if near-term earnings or known event risk
- Short-dated (<15d): must include jump term; purely diffusion formula fails here
