### New content added

**New source page**: `sources/dynamic-inclusion-bounded-tilts-2026.md` — arXiv:2601.05428, "Dynamic Inclusion and Bounded Multi-Factor Tilts for Robust Portfolio Construction."

Key mechanism extracted:
- **Dynamic inclusion eligibility gate**: `U_t = {i : H_i(t) ≥ H_min, ADV_bar_i(t) ≥ ADV_min}` — standard PIT-safe liquidity/history filter.
- **Bounded multiplicative tilt** (the novel piece): combine factor z-scores as a convex mixture `z_i(t) = Σ α_f z_{i,f}(t)`, convert to a bounded multiplier `m_i(t) = clip(1 + λ·z_i(t), m_min, m_max)`, then renormalize on top of an equal-weight base: `w_i(t) = w_i^EW(t)·m_i(t) / Σ_j w_j^EW(t)·m_j(t)`. When factor signal is weak the portfolio reverts smoothly to 1/N; when strong, weights tilt but can never exceed the bound — no discrete regime-switch cliff edge.
- Paper is a construction-mechanism paper, not an alpha paper — no Sharpe/CAGR/MaxDD numbers published, only qualitative claims (lower concentration, "improved drawdown convexity," lower turnover vs cap-weighted).

**Direct connection to tonight's earlier H478 result**: H478 (Golden Criterion, evaluated in last night's session, NOT CONFIRMED) tried a *binary* switch between top-1 concentration and EW-top-3/5 diversification gated on eigenvalue dispersion — all 5 variants failed, and the always-EW-top-5 variant was the best performer. This paper's bounded continuous tilt is a natural next iteration: replace the binary D-threshold switch with `w_i = EW_i · clip(1 + λ·mom_z_i, m_min, m_max)` applied across the full universe every month, avoiding the cliff-edge concentration switch that likely hurt H478. Flagged as a candidate next hypothesis in the wiki page's "Practical Implications" section — not staged as a full backtest script tonight (see Dream Cycle below for what was actually staged).
