# Research Log — 2026-05-11

## Phase 1: Wiki Expansion — Low-Volatility Anomaly

Created `wiki/trading/algorithms/low-volatility.md` — new strategy family page covering the three main low-vol implementations.

**Coverage:**

1. **Low-Volatility Decile (Blitz & Vliet 2007)**: Rank by 3-year rolling weekly return std; long bottom decile, monthly rebalance. US large-cap: Sharpe 0.72, CAGR 11.3%, MaxDD −45.9% (1986–2006). Anomaly mechanism: leverage constraints cause investors to overpay for high-beta stocks.

2. **Betting Against Beta (Frazzini & Pedersen 2014)**: Market-neutral factor — long low-beta stocks (leveraged to β=1), short high-beta (deleveraged to β=1). Beta = vol ratio × 5-year monthly correlation. US Stocks 1926–2012: Sharpe 0.78 — ~2× value factor, 40% more than momentum. Full Python implementation included.

3. **Minimum Variance Portfolio**: Full optimization via skfolio `MinimumVariance`. Clarke et al.: 25% lower realized volatility than cap-weighted index with comparable returns.

4. **Sector-Neutral variant**: Rank vol within GICS sector before forming portfolio — most crowding-resistant after SPLV/USMV absorbed naive low-vol alpha post-2011.

**Hypothesis queue added**: H191 (low-vol decile), H192 (BAB beta rank), H193 (sector-neutral low-vol), H194 (low-vol + H026 blend).

**Crowding note**: SPLV/USMV launch (~2011) has compressed raw anomaly alpha 30–50%. Sector-neutral and BAB variants show better post-crowding resilience.

**Cross-references**: Added to wiki/index.md and wiki/trading/index.md; cross-linked from Short-Term Reversal and Momentum Strategies families.
