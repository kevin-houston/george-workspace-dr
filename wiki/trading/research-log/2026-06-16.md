### Key findings added to wiki

1. **Cross-sectional momentum**: 28-day lookback, top-quartile long, top-50 by market cap universe → Sharpe 1.51. No skip-month needed (unlike equities). Inv-vol weighting critical.

2. **BTC trend following**: 50d MA or EMA-crossover → Sharpe 1.9 vs buy-and-hold 1.3 (2012–2023). 126% annualized returns with MA strategy. Strong academic backing.

3. **Funding rate carry**: Structurally 8% annualized return (2020–2025), Sharpe 6.45 — but Sharpe fell to 4.06 in 2024 and went negative in 2025 as ETF adoption compressed the premium. NOT a viable long-term strategy.

4. **Monday effect**: BTC Monday returns avg +0.51% (buy Sunday evening, sell Monday close). Negative weekend BTC returns predict Monday US equity declines (asymmetric loss aversion spillover).

5. **Halving cycle**: 4 halvings show 12–18 month lag to peak. Returns diminishing each cycle (7000% → 291% → 541% → 100%). 2024 halving peaked Oct 2025 — now in post-peak phase.

6. **Python stack**: ccxt + pycoingecko; full implementation code for cross-sectional momentum backtest, BTC MA strategy, universe builder with stablecoin filter, halving cycle position calculator.
