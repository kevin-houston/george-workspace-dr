Target: AI Industry (thinnest section at 10 pages, least cross-linked relative to depth of recent papers)

**New page created**: `wiki/ai-industry/base-rate-trap-llm-trading-2026.md`

Synthesizes three July 2026 papers exposing the base-rate trap in LLM trading signal evaluation:

1. **arXiv:2607.12248** (Cheung, Jul 2026): LoRA-adapted TimesFM showed 80% directional accuracy — identical to the naive "always-up" baseline in bull market conditions. The fine-tuned model was scoring **below the base rate**. Full honest-benchmark protocol: always-up + random walk + persistence + AR(1) + zero-shot as the required five baselines; McNemar test + Diebold-Mariano + BH FDR correction.

2. **arXiv:2607.05291** (Brini, Jul 2026): Foundation models for realized volatility barely beat Log-HAR econometric benchmark. Only TTM outperforms — by a narrow margin. Best practice: TTM + Log-HAR ensemble.

3. Synthesis with KTD-Fin (arXiv:2605.28359) and Agentic Trading Survey (arXiv:2605.19337): LLM alpha = momentum beta (Barra attribution); 0/19 papers fully reproducible; none used base-rate baseline.

**Why this matters for the pipeline**: H381/H382 LLM alpha mining proposals require base-rate testing before claiming alpha. H174 PEAD pipeline passes the test (81.8% WR vs ~60-65% base rate) but must be rechecked annually.
