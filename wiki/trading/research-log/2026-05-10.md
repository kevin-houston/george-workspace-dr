### H188 — 52-Week High Proximity Momentum (George & Hwang 2004)

**Status: CONFIRMED**

Signal: `prox_i = P_t / max(P_{t-252d:t-1})` — measures how close price is to its 52-week high. Based on anchoring bias: investors use the 52-week high as a reference point and resist buying near it, causing underreaction. Long top-6 stocks by proximity, monthly rebalance, same 30-stock GICS universe as H181.
