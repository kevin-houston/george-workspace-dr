## New content: market making & liquidity provision

New page `prediction-markets/market-making.md`. Checked the existing
`backtesting-frameworks.md` page first to avoid duplicating its PolyBot/arbitrage-bot
coverage — confirmed it documents general backtesting engines and one MCP agent
framework, not market-making specifically, so this is a genuinely new angle: **providing**
liquidity rather than taking it.

Covered:
- Why prediction-market MM structurally differs from equities MM — no continuous
  underlying to hedge, binary terminal payoff, inventory risk that explodes near
  resolution rather than diffusing away
- Avellaneda-Stoikov (2008) reservation-price/spread model adapted to the 0-1 bounded
  price space, with a working Python reference function (boundary-clipped quotes have
  no equities analog)
- arXiv:2607.17991 "Optimal Market Making in Prediction Markets" — HJB equation over a
  transformed latent-belief diffusion, fixes the naive-AS gap of not letting quote width
  scale with belief volatility near price extremes; no concrete Sharpe/PnL numbers were
  available at the abstract level, noted honestly rather than invented
- arXiv:2311.08725 general cost-function theory of liquidity provisioning (LMSR-adjacent)
- Cartea/Drissi/Monga "predictable loss" metric (SSRN:4273989) — flagged as paywalled,
  only the qualitative finding captured, not fabricated formulas
- Three open-source repos: `rodlaf/KalshiMarketMaker` (★195, live Avellaneda-Stoikov
  reference implementation with real-time dashboard), `nikhilnd/kalshi-market-making`,
  `orangejuicetin/kalshi_market_maker` (real live-trading provenance)
- Market context: Kalshi ~$5.8B / Polymarket ~$3.74B November 2025 monthly volume,
  maker-rebate revenue mechanism, latency requirements vs. this project's existing
  daily/monthly-rebalance cadence
