**Gap identified:** IBS (Internal Bar Strength) mean-reversion strategy is actively running in production at 30% of portfolio weight (XLK 20% / SMH 8% / IGV 2%) but had no dedicated wiki page. The H062–H149 hypothesis sequence developed and confirmed this strategy. The page was missing entirely.

**New page created:** `wiki/trading/algorithms/ibs-mean-reversion.md`

Key content captured:
- IBS formula: `(Close − Low) / (High − Low)` — positions the close within the daily range
- Academic grounding: Pandey & Joshi arXiv:2306.12434 (Sharpe up to 3.9 on country ETFs); Pagonidis 2013 IBS Effect paper
- Standard literature parameters: buy < 0.2, sell > 0.8
- **Production parameters** drawn from run_h149.py source code:
  - XLK: buy=0.15, sell=0.90, hold=7, gap≥−1.0% (slow bouncer, negative gap OK)
  - SMH: buy=0.20, sell=0.75, hold=6, gap≥−0.5% (faster exit)
  - IGV: buy=0.30, sell=0.75, hold=5, gap≥+0.25% (positive gap required — different regime)
- H062–H149 hypothesis development table (10 entries)
- Limitations: doji handling, gap-open risk, trending regime underperformance, commodity ETF inapplicability
- H149 result: 70%/30% rotation/IBS split confirmed optimal; pure H026 underperforms the blend
