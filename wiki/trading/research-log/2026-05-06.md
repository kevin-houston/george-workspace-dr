# Research Log 2026-05-06

## Phase 1 — PEAD-NLP Live Deployment Wiki

**Thinnest section:** paper-trading/ (210 lines total; no coverage for event-driven strategies).

**New page:** `paper-trading/pead-nlp-alpaca.md` (~250 lines)

Documents the full pipeline for deploying the confirmed H163/H174 PEAD-NLP strategy to Alpaca paper trading:

- **Alpaca WebSocket streaming** (`StockDataStream` + `subscribe_bars`): gap-up detection at 9:30 AM open using `wss://stream.data.alpaca.markets/v2/iex`; bar format `{"T":"b","o":open,"c":close,"t":timestamp,...}`.
- **EDGAR 8-K overnight poller**: EFTS full-text search endpoint `https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q="results+of+operations"&forms=8-K&dateRange=custom&startdt=...`; edgartools v5 `Company.get_filings(form="8-K").latest().obj()`; typical latency: after-hours earners file 8-K by 6–8 PM ET (same day) → available for overnight scoring.
- **FinBERT scoring + surprise filter**: wrapper for H163/H174 pipeline; score ≥ 0.18 (primary), surprise ≥ 0.02 (secondary).
- **Alpaca order submission**: `MarketOrderRequest(notional=..., time_in_force=TimeInForce.OPG)` for next-open entry; separate `StopOrderRequest(GTC)` for stop-loss (fractional shares can't use bracket orders); `TimeInForce.CLS` (MOC) for day-20 exit.
- **OPG vs market order timing**: If 8-K available by 5 AM ET, OPG order placed before 9:28 AM cutoff fills at exact open. Otherwise, detect gap in first bar and submit market order.
- **Position sizing**: 5% of equity per event; 10% stop-loss; 20-day hold; co-exists with H149 rotation (PEAD = event-driven, H149 = monthly).

**Key discovery:** Alpaca fractional shares (`notional=` parameter) cannot use bracket orders — stop-loss must be a separate GTC stop order submitted after entry fills.
