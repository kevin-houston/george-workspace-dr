# Research Log — 2026-05-08

## Phase 1: Wiki expansion — Sector & Industry Classification

**Identified gap**: H181 (industry-adjusted short-term reversal) requires per-stock GICS/SIC industry codes. No wiki page existed for sector classification data sources.

**New page**: `data-sources/sector-classification.md`

Key findings:
- **SEC EDGAR SIC codes** (free, bulk, point-in-time via `data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK{CIK}.json`) — best option for historical backtests; SIC→GICS mapping table included
- **GitHub datasets/s-and-p-500-companies** — current S&P 500 with GICS sectors, instant free CSV; not point-in-time but acceptable for our stable large-cap universe
- **yfinance `.info['sector']`** — current data only, NOT suitable for historical backtests; acceptable for fixed universe where sector is stable
- **FMP API** — custom classification (not GICS), 250 calls/day free, paid tier needed for bulk
- **Polygon.io** — SIC codes but 5 calls/min rate limit makes bulk lookup impractical on free tier
- **Static SPDR ETF mapping** — practical 30-ticker universe mapping included for our current PEAD/H181 universe
- **`build_sector_cache()`** utility function written: tries GitHub CSV first (GICS), falls back to SEC EDGAR SIC, caches to parquet

**H181 implementation decision**: For our 30-100 large-cap universe, current GICS from GitHub CSV is accurate (sectors stable 2019-2026 for large-caps). Full point-in-time approach (Wikipedia revisions + EDGAR) only needed for dynamic universe > 200 stocks.

## Phase 2: Dream cycle — arXiv/GitHub scan

### TOP FINDINGS

**1. arXiv:2511.12490 — 13-Sharpe OOS Factor (Singha, Nov 2025)** ★★★★★
> "Drift-regime conditioning on value + short-term reversal achieves 13.2 OOS Sharpe (CAGR 158.6%, MaxDD -11.9%) over 2004-2024."

Drift regime: >60% positive days in trailing 63-day window. Walk-forward validated, 1,000 randomization trials. This directly extends H181 — the industry-adjusted reversal baseline (0.53%/month from SSRN:6630998) may be dramatically enhanced by regime conditioning. **Staged as H182 proposal.**

**2. arXiv:2509.24254 — Press Release Structure Predicts Earnings Returns (ICAIF 2025)** ★★★★☆
> "138,000+ earnings press releases (2005-2023): soft information is as informative as earnings surprises. FinBERT outperforms LDA."

Independently validates H163/H174 (deployed in paper trading). Strong academic backing for our confirmed strategy. **Staged as wiki update for event-driven.md.**

**3. ModernFinBERT (HuggingFace, July 2025)** ★★★☆☆
> Successor to ProsusAI/finbert, based on ModernBERT architecture, specialized for earnings calls and analyst reports.

Drop-in upgrade candidate for H163/H174 scoring pipeline. Low cost (~2h to validate). **Staged as H176 wiki note.**

**4. arXiv:2501.07135 — Network Momentum (Lim et al., Jan 2025)** ★★★★☆
> "Lead-lag cross-sectional momentum via inter-market connectivity outperforms univariate trend-following."

Addresses the gap left by H157 (factor ETF rotation NOT CONFIRMED): network momentum works at daily frequency with spillover between related stocks. New hypothesis H183 queued. **Staged as H183 proposal.**

**5. arXiv:2601.06499 — 17 Alpha191 Factors in US Market (2026)** ★★★☆☆

Validates short-term cross-sectional predictability in 2026 US data. Useful reference when H156/H158 revisited with unbiased universe data.

**6. H165 (TradingAgents) ecosystem** ★★★☆☆
