## Hypothesis testing

### H200 — Graphical Matching Pairs Trading — NOT CONFIRMED

- Source: arXiv:2403.07998 (Qureshi & Zaman 2024)
- Universe: 30 large-cap S&P 500 stocks, daily 2013–2026
- Method: networkx `max_weight_matching` on correlation graph → Engle-Granger cointegration filter (p < 0.05) → daily z-score pairs trading
- Result: **0/15 pairs cointegrated** in IS first-year sample; OOS Sharpe -1.143, Cumul 0.41×
- Diagnosis: EG test rejects all pairs on large-cap US stocks. Consistent with H152–H160 ETF pairs failures.
- **Pairs family EXHAUSTED** — both ETF pairs (H152–H160) and stock-level pairs (H200) failed the cointegration filter. Next pairs angle would require an alternative spread measure (e.g. copula, neural ODE).

### H201 — Turn-of-Month Effect — CONFIRMED ✓

- Source: Ariel (1987); Lakonishok & Smidt (1988); §3.6 "151 Trading Strategies"
- Universe: SPY, daily 2003–2026; IS 2003–2017, OOS 2018–2026
- Method: Hold SPY during last N + first M trading days of each month, BIL otherwise. Window optimized in IS.
- Best window: last-2 + first-2 trading days (4 days/month, ~18% of trading days)
- IS Sharpe: 0.147 (weak — but TOM premium strengthened post-2018)
- **OOS Sharpe: 0.740, Cumul: 1.559×, MaxDD: -9.3%**
- TOM premium: +0.097%/day vs -0.004%/day outside window
- Corr with SPY OOS: 0.75 (directional exposure, not market-neutral)
- Confirmed thresholds met: Sharpe > 0.5 ✓, Cumul > 1.3× ✓
- Note: Inverted IS/OOS pattern — TOM effect appears to have strengthened post-2018 (institutional rebalancing flows increased). Not a data-mining concern — effect is academically established for 35 years.
