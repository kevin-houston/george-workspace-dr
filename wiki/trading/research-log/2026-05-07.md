## Wiki expansion: Walk-Forward Validation & CPCV

**Target**: `backtesting/` section — thinnest in the wiki (1 substantive page before tonight).

**New page**: `backtesting/walk-forward-cpcv.md`

Covers:
- Why standard k-fold CV fails for financial time series (temporal leakage, single-path variance, multiple testing)
- Anchored vs rolling WFO with ASCII diagrams
- CPCV algorithm from López de Prado Ch. 12: partition → enumerate C(N,k) combinations → purge → embargo → evaluate
- Purging logic (remove training samples whose label horizon overlaps test window)
- Embargoing logic (buffer after test block, typically 5–10%)
- C(N,k) path counts and computational cost warnings (N=30,k=4 → 27k paths)
- Deflated Sharpe Ratio formulas (PSR → DSR), impact table by M trials
- Python libraries: `timeseriescv` (MIT, lightweight), `skfolio` (BSD-3, portfolio-focused), `mlfinlab` (production, DSR integration)
- Practical parameter guide (N=10–25, k=2–3, embargo=5–10%, minimum 100 paths for stability)
- Connection to current pipeline: H026's ~100 variants imply DSR deflates Sharpe ~3.0→~2.0; dual OOS window is a practical proxy

**Key finding from 2024 literature** (Arian et al.): CPCV shows significantly lower Probability of Backtest Overfitting than k-fold, purged k-fold, and single-path WFO in a controlled synthetic benchmark. CPCV is the correct method before live capital deployment of any ML-fitted strategy in the pipeline (H172, H176).
