## New page: paper-trading/corporate-actions-handling.md

Identified a genuine, previously-undocumented operational gap: none of the
paper-trading scripts (`h112_monthly.py`, `h181_monthly.py`, `pead_*` family)
reconcile cash dividends, splits, or symbol changes against
`strategy_accounts.json`. Every ETF/stock sleeve in the production portfolio
holds dividend-paying instruments across a full rebalance cycle, and the
gap risks desyncing sleeve-tracked equity from what Alpaca's account
actually reflects — which would corrupt the OOS-vs-live comparison the
Live Graduation Criteria gate depends on.

Researched via Alpaca's own blog post, SDK reference, and API docs pages.
Key findings written up:
- **Alpaca Corporate Actions API**: `GET data.alpaca.markets/v1/corporate-actions`,
  replaces the now-deprecated `TradingClient.get_corporate_announcements()`.
  14 supported types (`cash_dividend`, `forward_split`, `reverse_split`,
  `name_change`, `spin_off`, mergers, etc.), historical depth back to April
  2020, new data lands before market open the day after declaration date.
- **Notable gap**: Alpaca's own announcement explicitly flags symbol
  changes/redemptions/delistings as less reliably covered than scheduled
  dividends/splits — worth defensive handling in any script keying
  positions by ticker string.
- **Code example**: `alpaca-py`'s `CorporateActionsClient.get_corporate_actions()`
  fetching upcoming dividends for the full H026 11-sector-ETF universe, plus
  a raw `curl` equivalent.
- **Pricing tier**: not clearly documented for this specific endpoint —
  flagged to verify empirically against the paper account rather than
  assume free-tier access, since it lives under the same `data.alpaca.markets`
  host as the paid Algo Trader Plus ($99/mo) market-data surface.
- **Practical reconciliation pattern proposed** (not yet built): (1)
  dividends auto-credit to Alpaca cash, but per-sleeve NAV tracking needs a
  matching credit query each morning; (2) splits auto-adjust `qty` on
  Alpaca's side — audit scripts for stale cached `shares_held` rather than
  live-queried position qty; (3) daily `name_change` check with an alert
  (not silent skip) since ticker remaps need human/agent judgment.
