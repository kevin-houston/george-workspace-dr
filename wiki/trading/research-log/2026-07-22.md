### Key Insight Documented

IBS does NOT exploit directional autocorrelation (the sign channel is noise, p=0.11). It exploits **magnitude compression**: closes cluster toward the middle of the day's range because bid-ask bounce and liquidity replenishment are asymmetric — sell-side pushes during the session are followed by stronger buy-side responses. IBS < 0.20 identifies days where sell-side dominated; the mean-reversion is driven by liquidity mechanics, not trader sentiment or momentum.
