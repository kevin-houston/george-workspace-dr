## New content: LLM sentiment + risk decomposition, small-cap trading

New page `sources/llm-sentiment-risk-decomposition-smallcap-2026.md` covering
arXiv:2608.12283 (Kargarzadeh, Khaledian, Parvini & Khaledian, submitted Aug 12
2026 — genuinely within the recency window, verified not a duplicate of any
existing wiki page via full-text search first).

Covered:
- Full methodology: GPT-4o mini sentiment scoring with single-linkage
  agglomerative de-duplication (0.90 cosine threshold) against syndicated/wire
  articles, entity-prior correction, point-in-time-safe cross-sectional z-scoring
- The paper's central structural finding — splitting stock-selection into
  **pure-alpha** (stock moved, no macro co-trigger) vs. **pure-beta** (macro
  indicator fired, stock hasn't reacted yet) vs. intersection, with the
  intersection regime *consistently underperforming* either pure trigger alone
- Aleatoric + epistemic risk decomposition (dropout-based multimodal net, 50
  stochastic passes) feeding portfolio covariance directly — a model-uncertainty-
  aware sizing mechanism distinct from anything in position-sizing.md
- Full results table: best cell (pure-beta, GPT-4o mini, 40-day hold, risk-parity)
  OOS Sharpe 2.33 / MaxDD -18.3% at 100bp costs; FinBERT/Mistral-7B/Llama-2-13B
  scorer comparison
- Caveats stated plainly: single calendar-year (2025) OOS only, Russell 2000
  small-cap universe, boutique/non-academic affiliation (Tailstate Intelligence /
  Zanista AI) — treated with the same skepticism this wiki applied to AlphaZeroBeta

Cross-referenced into three existing pages rather than left as an orphan:
- `algorithms/pead.md` — new Research Lead entry proposing a pure-alpha/pure-beta
  re-slice of the existing 22-event H174 OOS sample (flagged that n will likely
  drop below the usual n>=20 gate once split, so viable only as a qualitative
  check for now)
- `algorithms/llm-alpha-validation.md` — added to the Cross-References list as a
  live example to run through the checklist's single-year-OOS/affiliation
  skepticism criteria before any hypothesis is staged from it
- `tools/nlp-alternative-data.md` — new short section on the de-duplication
  clustering step as a portable, low-effort fix for the existing FinBERT/OPT
  scoring pipeline (pead_overnight.py), independent of the paper's larger claims
