### High-Relevance Findings

**1. arXiv:2511.12490 — "Discovery of a 13-Sharpe OOS Factor: Drift Regimes Unlock Hidden Cross-Sectional Predictability" (Singha, Nov 2025)**

Independent academic validation of the H411/H416 drift-regime mechanism:
- S&P 500 universe 2004-2024, walk-forward validation with frozen parameters
- **63-day drift window, 60% positive-day threshold** — remarkably close to H411's 20d/60% design
- Base signal: 0.7 × (1/price rank) + 0.3 × (−10d reversal z-score) — 1/price is H411's primary signal
- EDGE = BASE × REGIME (deactivate when not in drift regime — H411's same logic)
- Daily long-short rebalancing → OOS Sharpe 13.19, CAGR 158.6%, MaxDD -11.9%
- Strong VIX-conditional breakdown: Sharpe 18.3 (VIX<15) → 2.1 (VIX≥35)
- **H424 staged**: monthly long-only version on S&P 500 universe; expected realistic Sharpe 1.5-3.0

**2. arXiv:2607.04958 — "Look-Ahead-Freedom as Temporal Non-Interference" (Fonseca, Jul 2026)**

Formal CS verification of look-ahead bias detection:
- Establishes decidability boundary: value-independent fragment decidable in O(n) time
- Type-and-effect system catches indirect leaks (cached intermediates, agentic retrieval) that statistical detectors miss

- **New wiki page**: `wiki/trading/backtesting/lookahead-formal-verification.md`

### Medium-Relevance Findings
