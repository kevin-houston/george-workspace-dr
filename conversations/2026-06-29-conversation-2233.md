**What it is:** Python library implementing ICT (Inner Circle Trader) price action concepts — the "smart money" methodology popular in retail trading circles. `pip install smartmoneyconcepts`, 1,788 stars, clean vectorized pandas/numpy, drops in directly with yfinance data.

**8 indicators:**
- **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** — price imbalance where the prior high < next low (or vice versa); theory is institutions left unfilled orders there and price returns to fill it
- **Order Blocks (OB)** — consolidation zone immediately before a strong impulse; ICT theory says institutions accumulated/distributed there and it acts as future S/R
- **Break of Structure (BOS)** — continuation signal: price breaks a prior swing high/low
- **Change of Character (CHoCH)** — reversal signal: price breaks *against* recent structure
- **Liquidity** — cluster of nearby swing highs/lows where stop losses concentrate; smart money "sweeps" these before reversing
- **Sessions** — marks candles in London/NY/Tokyo/kill zones; relevant for intraday timing
- Plus Swing Highs/Lows and Retracements
