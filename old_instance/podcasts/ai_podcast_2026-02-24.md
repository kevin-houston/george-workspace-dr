## The Persona Selection Model: Why AIs Act Human

**Alex:** Now let's talk about Anthropic's fascinating new research on why AI assistants like Claude seem so human.

**Jordan:** They published a post called "The Persona Selection Model" that explains why AIs express emotions, use anthropomorphic language, and develop distinct personalities.

**Alex:** The theory is that during training, the model learns from text written by humans playing different roles—helpful assistants, experts, storytellers, fictional characters.

**Jordan:** So when you ask Claude or ChatGPT a question, it's not just retrieving information. It's selecting which persona best fits the context and responding as that persona would.

**Alex:** This explains why AIs can seem empathetic, enthusiastic, or cautious depending on the situation. They're pattern-matching to human behaviors they saw during training.

**Jordan:** But here's where it gets interesting from a safety perspective. Anthropic says "if AIs inherit traits from fictional role models, we should give them as good role models as possible."

**Alex:** That's why Claude's constitution matters. They're literally trying to give the AI good examples to learn from, the same way you'd be careful about what influences a child.

**Jordan:** It's a fascinating blend of psychology and machine learning. We're not just programming behaviors—we're shaping personalities through the training data.

**Alex:** And the post got nearly 500,000 views and 1,700 bookmarks. This is hitting a nerve with people trying to understand how to work with AI.

**Jordan:** Because once you understand that the AI is selecting a persona, you can be more intentional about which persona you're invoking with your prompts.

## EVMbench: AI Agents for Smart Contract Security

**Alex:** Let's talk about OpenAI's new benchmark for AI agents in blockchain security.

**Jordan:** EVMbench measures how well AI agents can detect, exploit, and patch high-severity vulnerabilities in smart contracts on the Ethereum Virtual Machine.

**Alex:** This is important because billions of dollars are locked in smart contracts, and vulnerabilities can lead to massive hacks.

**Jordan:** The fact that they're testing for both exploitation AND patching is interesting. The AI needs to think like both an attacker and a defender.

**Alex:** This connects to the security themes we saw in yesterday's curated podcast. As AI agents get more autonomous, security becomes critical.

**Jordan:** And using AI to secure AI-powered systems is going to be a major category. You can't manually audit every smart contract—there are too many.

**Alex:** 2.3 million views on this announcement. The blockchain community clearly cares about AI-powered security.

**Jordan:** Especially given all the high-profile hacks we've seen. If AI can automatically find and fix vulnerabilities before they're exploited, that's a game-changer.
