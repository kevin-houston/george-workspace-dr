   Avg Sharpe: 0.533 | Avg vs BH: -0.58%/yr | Premium: 18.6%/yr

   Good for: KO, MO, T, PG, VZ (slow/no-growth names)
   Bad for: IBM, XOM, CVX (high growth or energy vol)

2. CASH-SECURED PUTS (monthly, ATM, 30d)
   Avg Sharpe: 0.210 | Avg vs Cash: -2.4%/yr | Premium: 28.7%/yr
   Best: IBM (0.449), XOM (0.418)
   Note: ATM puts collect more premium but face worse assignment losses
   Result: Inferior to covered calls on Sharpe despite higher income

3. EARNINGS STRADDLE (gap events as proxy, 30-stock PEAD universe)
   5d_before: Sharpe 6.02, WR 62.2%, avg +1.83%/trade
   2d_before: Sharpe 3.79, WR 62.9%, avg +0.89%/trade
   1d_before: Sharpe 3.89, WR 68.4%, avg +0.55%/trade
   Optimal: 5 days before event
   CAVEAT: Sharpes inflated ~10x due to gap-event look-ahead bias;
           realistic live Sharpe ~0.3-0.5 after IV crush adjustments

4. PROTECTIVE PUT ON PEAD (5% OTM put, 20d, gap>5% events)
   PEAD raw Sharpe: 4.463
   PEAD + put Sharpe: 0.251 (SEVERELY DEGRADED)
   Avg put cost: 3.57%/trade
   Verdict: DESTROYS EDGE. Do not add protective puts to PEAD trades.
   The ~3.6% put premium exceeds PEAD alpha in most cases.

5. VIX SHORT VOL (VXX -5% signal, hold 5d, 0.5% simulated premium)
   Sharpe: -4.975 | Win rate: 68.3% | Avg return: -3.34%/trade
   Max DD: -99.6%
   Verdict: UNPROFITABLE. 68% win rate overwhelmed by catastrophic losses
   on VIX spike events. VXX structural decay pattern does not support
   reliable short-vol extraction at 0.5% premium level.

KEY FINDING:
Covered calls provide modest risk-adjusted improvement for range-bound
dividend stocks. The premium income story (18-29%/yr) is compelling but
partially illusory after assignment losses. Never add protective puts to
PEAD — the 3.6%/trade insurance premium wipes the entire edge. VXX
short-vol fails catastrophically in a world with episodic VIX spikes.
The options strategy that works best alongside PEAD would be covered
calls on the same positions during the 20-day hold period, though this
was not directly tested.
