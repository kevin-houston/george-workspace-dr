### Critical Insight from R26 (LLM Signal Filtering)
The most important research finding this week: LLM signal filtering HURTS PEAD performance. The signals the IndicatorAgent would REJECT (Sharpe 0.904) outperform those it CONFIRMS (Sharpe 0.716). This is because PEAD is fundamentally anti-IndicatorAgent — PEAD fires on "overbought" gaps that indicator-based scoring penalizes. This clarifies the appropriate use of LLM layers:
- LLM as NARRATIVE GENERATOR = valuable (explainability, product feature)
- LLM as SIGNAL FILTER on event-driven strategies = harmful
- LLM as SIGNAL FILTER on pure technical patterns = potentially useful (untested)
