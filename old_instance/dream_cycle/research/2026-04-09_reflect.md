## Key Gaps Identified in Tonight's Scan

### Gap 1: Metric Shifting as Missing EarningsQualityAgent Signal
The R28 design currently scores: (1) Specificity, (2) Sentiment, (3) Risk density. But arXiv:2510.03195 reveals a FOURTH signal dimension: **metric shifting** — what metrics did management STOP mentioning compared to last quarter? High metric shifting (moving targets) predicts NEGATIVE abnormal returns (-0.52%/month 5-factor alpha). This is the "what are they hiding?" signal. The other three dimensions focus on the CURRENT call; metric shifting compares to the PRIOR call.

This is easy to implement: extract metric list from current call, compare to prior call metric list using embedding similarity, compute a "new metrics introduced / prior metrics dropped" score.

### Gap 2: LLM Extrapolation Bias in Prompting (arXiv:2604.02921)
Bare LLM prompts over-extrapolate recent stock trends when making predictions. This is a previously unknown mechanism explaining why R26's IndicatorAgent failed — the LLM was seeing recent price momentum and extrapolating it, rather than evaluating earnings quality. This validates the RAG-grounding lesson but adds a new nuance: even with RAG, LLM prompts may over-weight recent performance when asked to score "earnings quality." The fix: explicitly instruct the LLM to ignore recent price action in the EarningsQualityAgent prompt.

### Gap 3: Strategy-Decay Risk Diagnostic (arXiv:2604.08356)
MRP (Minimum Regime Performance) is a new tool for evaluating backtest durability. Our library has 146 strategies across 30+ rounds. The top strategies (PEAD Sharpe 4.46, Div Raise Sharpe 4.403) may have poor MRP if they were tuned on specific bull-market regimes. This is a diagnostic pass worth doing on the library.

### Gap 4: RegimeGuard Upgrade (arXiv:2603.04441)
The current R28 RegimeGuard uses VIX > 30 and SMA crossover. Wasserstein HMM (arXiv:2603.04441) achieves much cleaner regime identification. However, this is medium complexity — defer to concept note, not implementation stage.
