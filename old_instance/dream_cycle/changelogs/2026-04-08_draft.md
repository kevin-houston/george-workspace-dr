## Summary

Tonight's scan focused on R29 (pairs trading LLM filter) and R28 (EarningsQualityAgent design), which are the next two rounds to run. Two high-quality findings emerged:

1. **SAE Company Similarity** (ACL 2025, arXiv:2412.02605) — A peer-reviewed, code-available method that uses Sparse Autoencoder features from Llama 3.1 8B to cluster companies for pairs trading. Achieves Sharpe 12.18 vs. SIC codes 9.70 on S&P 500 out-of-sample. Pre-computed features on HuggingFace mean no GPU needed. Staged as R29 Stage 1.5 amendment.

2. **Analyst Belief Biases in Earnings Calls** (arXiv:2511.15214) — Analysts over-react to sentiment, under-react to risk language. This validates a 3-part scoring approach for R28 EarningsQualityAgent: specificity (50%), sentiment (30%), risk density inverted (20%). More theoretically grounded than single composite score.

Also found **Retail Investor Horizon → PEAD signal** (arXiv:2512.00280): long-horizon investor composition amplifies PEAD. Staged as R34 concept using 13-F institutional ownership as free proxy.

3. **Manager Evasiveness in Earnings Calls** (arXiv:2505.18419, May 2025) — LLM-detectable non-responses (NORs) in earnings call Q&A amplify PEAD. High NOR rate = more analyst uncertainty = more drift to come. Staged as PEAD position-sizing modifier for R28 (upsize by 25% when NOR > 25%). Implementable with open-source LLaMA 3.3.

## Key Research Findings

### 1. SAE Company Similarity (arXiv:2412.02605, ACL 2025)
- Best pairs selection method found: Sharpe **12.18** (vs SIC 9.70, embeddings 7.58-10.57)
- R29 Stage 1.5: Use SAE clusters as fast deterministic pre-filter before LLM plausibility scoring
- Free HuggingFace dataset: `marco-molinari/company_reports_with_features`
- Code: https://github.com/FlexCode29/company_similarity_sae

### 2. Analyst Belief Asymmetry (arXiv:2511.15214, Nov 2025)
- Over-react to optimism, under-react to risk language
- R28 Amendment: 3-part EarningsQualityAgent scoring:
  - Specificity (50% weight) — most predictive dimension
  - Sentiment (30%) — already over-incorporated by analysts
  - Risk density inverted (20%) — under-priced signal

### 3. Manager Evasiveness / NOR Signal (arXiv:2505.18419, May 2025)
- NOR (non-response rate) in Q&A earnings calls detectable via 3-step LLM prompting
- High NOR → MORE PEAD drift (not less) — it's a PEAD amplifier, not quality filter
- R28 Amendment: NOR rate > 25% + quality_score > 50 → upsize PEAD position 25%
- Free implementation: LLaMA 3.3 (open-source) on already-extracted Q&A section
- Effect magnified for high institutional ownership firms (consistent with R34 concept)

### 5. TradingAgents v0.2.3 + Earnings Call Models
- TradingAgents v0.2.3 (Mar 29): backtesting date fidelity fix + unified model catalog. Use for R28.
- NLPScholars/Roberta-Earning-Call-Transcript-Classification: multi-label (litigious/constraining/uncertain), F1 82.8%, sentence-level
- Longformer (SeanD103): 4,096 token context — processes full transcripts without FinBERT chunking

### 4. Retail Investor Horizon + PEAD (arXiv:2512.00280, Dec 2025)
- Long-horizon retail → persistent PEAD; short-horizon → reversal
- 0.43%/month alpha from horizon heterogeneity zero-cost portfolio
- R34 concept: Use 13-F institutional ownership as free proxy signal
