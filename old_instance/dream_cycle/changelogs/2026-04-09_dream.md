### 2. R28 Amendment — Anti-Extrapolation Instruction in LLM Prompts
- **File:** `/workspace/group/MEMORY.md`
- **Source:** arXiv:2604.02921 (April 2026) — Debiasing LLMs by Fine-tuning
- **Summary:** LLMs over-extrapolate recent stock price trends when asked to evaluate quality. This caused R26 failure (LLM penalized gapped-up stocks). Zero-cost fix: explicit prompt instruction to ignore recent price/momentum in all EarningsQualityAgent calls. Generalizes to any LLM used for fundamental quality scoring in event-driven strategies.
