Signal Logic
------------
Week W-1 ("green bar"):  return ≥ 2.5%, volume ≥ 1.5× 20-wk avg, close in top 40% of range
Week W ("inside bar"):   high < W-1 high, low > W-1 low, close in upper 50% of W-1 bar range
Entry: open of week W+1  |  Exit: close of week W+1 (1-week hold)
Transaction cost: 0.10% round-trip  |  Max concurrent positions: 10

Interpretation
--------------
H234 confirms that the "coiled spring" pattern — a strong surging week followed by an
inside consolidation bar — predicts upside breakouts at weekly frequency in large-cap US
equities. Remarkably, the strategy gets STRONGER out-of-sample (OOS Sharpe 1.77 vs IS
Sharpe 0.86), with win rate improving from 53% to 64%. This is the opposite of the
typical IS/OOS degradation seen in most quantitative strategies.

The primary caution is drawdown: MaxDD of -47% OOS reflects the concentrated 1-week
directional nature of the strategy — when the market moves against a batch of active
positions, losses compound quickly. The high CAGR figures are driven by the equal-weight
averaging across trades and would be moderated by realistic portfolio-level position sizing.

This strategy is suitable as a standalone aggressive-growth satellite allocation, NOT as
a replacement for the production portfolio (H026/H041a/H045). Recommend small position
sizing (1-2% per trade max) if deploying live.
